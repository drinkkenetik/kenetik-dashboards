## Hypothesis

Pausing the 0.00x-ROAS underperformers on FB (whitelisting + specific Cleetus sub-segments [src: cgo-logs/daily-syntheses/2026-04-20-s1.md#D04-paid-media]) and redeploying the reclaimed ~$4,500/week to Google prospecting at ~3× current bid will yield net contribution margin improvement under any Google ROAS ≥2.0x (breakeven at 50% DTC margin). The hypothesis explicitly acknowledges that Google non-brand is DETERIORATING (−28% WoW, tROAS lockout active [src: cgo-logs/daily-syntheses/2026-04-20-s1.md#dept-paid-media-brief]) — this CS tests whether Google's portfolio-level 9.16x signal holds, reverses further, or accelerates downward under scaled spend. Either outcome is informative: a hold validates Google scale-up; a further decline confirms the non-brand deterioration thesis and triggers rollback via circuit-breaker.

## Expected Impact

**Methodology:** This is a reallocation CS — no net spend change. Per changeset-common-functions.md §3, incremental margin for a pure reallocation = incremental revenue × destination margin − Δ ad spend. Δ ad spend = $0 here (pause savings = new Google spend). Baseline assumption: paused Meta segments are at 0.00x ROAS per synthesis, so pause-side revenue loss = $0.

- **L1 margin:** Expected contribution margin gain of **$11,250 over 14d** (revenue.expected $22,500 × 50% DTC margin − $0 Δ spend). Downside $4,000 (at $8,000 incremental revenue, ~0.89x blended — below breakeven, Google deterioration materializes); upside $20,500 (at $41,000 incremental revenue, portfolio 9x holds). Prior draft's "$2.5k/wk net margin" and $13,500 expected envelope used a 60% margin rate that is not documented in §3 and double-counted the pause side — superseded by this calculation.
- **L2 cash:** Positive — no net spend change, better cash efficiency (reclaimed spend from 0x returns moves to a surface with expected >4x return band).
- **L3 customer quality:** Neutral-positive — Google prospecting audience in the Freedom Segment target set has historically converted at DTC subscription-attach rates comparable to Meta prospecting [src: brain/kenetik-brain/references/segments-personas.md#freedom-segment]. No direct Performance Memory prior on Google intent vs other channels exists at this time.
- **L4 learning:** Tests whether Google portfolio 9.16x signal holds, reverses, or accelerates downward under ~3× scaled spend into an actively-deteriorating non-brand environment. Either direction is informative; a further decline confirms the non-brand deterioration thesis and informs whether Google scale-up is dead for the remainder of the quarter.
- **L5 brand check:** **pass** — no creative, copy, or messaging change. Budget shift only. No new claims.

**Revenue envelope (14d incremental):** downside $8,000 (Google ~0.89x blended) / expected $22,500 (Google ~2.5x blended) / upside $41,000 (portfolio ~4.6x blended over 14d)
**Margin envelope (14d incremental, per §3 reallocation formula):** downside $4,000 / expected $11,250 / upside $20,500
_Note: Margin recomputed 2026-04-21 as incremental_revenue × 50% DTC margin − $0 Δ spend (pure reallocation; pause side at 0.00x = $0 revenue lost). Prior draft used 60% margin on gross revenue which is not documented in §3 and overstated the envelope by ~20%._
