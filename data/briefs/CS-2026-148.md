## §10a Hypothesis (expanded)

The Customer Winback flow has held 0% CVR for 7d on 3,456 recipients with a 29.6% open / 0.38% click pattern. This is the structural fingerprint of a creative/offer/LP problem, not audience fatigue: opens are healthy, clicks lapse, conversions die. Sub Winback (90 rec / 0 conv) and Browse Abandonment (374 rec / 0 conv) follow the same shape. The 0% rate is also dragging suppression-rate exposure higher and risking inbox-placement degradation if not paused/rebuilt within the 7d window.

The persistent VoC theme is price-affordability ("wish kenetik wasn't so expensive"; "It's a little pricey") on 5-star reviews — the ENGAGED audience likes the product but bounces on cost. A rebuilt winback flow with explicit value-stack + per-serving framing is the corrective action, not a second send-cadence experiment.

## §10b Resolution Path

- **Diagnose:** 7d 0% CVR pattern × 3 flows + price-affordability VoC theme × ENGAGED audience.
- **Remove:** PAUSE all 3 flows immediately to halt suppression accumulation.
- **Refine:** Existing flow templates almost handle this; rebuild creative + value-stack offer + route to post-CS-138 LP.
- **Add:** Net-new is only the Klaviyo send-time A/B if pause+rebuild is insufficient.
