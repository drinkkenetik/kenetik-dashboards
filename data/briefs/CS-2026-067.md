## 7. Payload — After

**Mechanic (revised 2026-04-27, twice):**
- Two parallel **2-touch** sequences delivered as **Klaviyo campaigns** (one-time scheduled sends to fixed cohort segments), **NOT a triggered flow**.
- The cohort is finite (1,460 profiles, defined by event "received Free 3 Pack on 2/3/26 and no purchase since"), so calendar-scheduled campaigns are simpler to operate, easier to measure (cohort-level T0 aligned to a single calendar date), and avoid the ongoing-trigger overhead of a flow.
- Cohort segments are suppressed from all other promotional campaigns during the 7-day window. Campaign 3 + Campaign 4 also suppress anyone who already converted from their track's Campaign 1 (Smart Sending exclusion on `Placed Order` event in last 7 days).
- Track B (D0) and Track A (D2) sends are staggered for operational deliverability cadence (roman@ is a new sender; warmup pacing) and cleaner measurement isolation. The cadence is not framed to customers as early access. Either track could collapse to a same-day send if preferred (operational, not strategic).

**Shared elements across all 4 campaigns:**
- **Sender:** From `roman@drinkkenetik.com` with display name "Roman" — Kenetik-owned domain anchors to brand while creator-named sender carries personal authority. CAN-SPAM compliant footer (physical address + unsubscribe link, standard Klaviyo template). FTC §255 material connection disclosure required in footer (Roman is a Kenetik equity partner — see [Sender & Compliance Setup](#sender--compliance-setup-added-2026-04-27)).
- **Voice:** Roman POV across both touches, written in his register, tone-reviewed by Nick before publish.
- **Hero product:** Kenetik Cans Variety 12-pack.
- **Suppressions:** Soft Bounces, Dead List, Inactive-to-Suppress, Hard Bounce 1+, Sunset Flow Subscribers, Placed Order last 7 days.
