## Graduation Rationale
This post clears the **Universal Reach threshold (>3x 30-day average)** by a wide margin — 8.1x, well above the 3x gate. Engagement rate (2.19%) does not clear the IG 5% bar, and save rate (0.05%) is below the 2% bar, so this is a **reach-qualified graduation, not an engagement-qualified one**. The signal here is that the Matt Hughes longevity-over-combat narrative is reaching — and being distributed by — an unusually wide audience. Paid amplification should be framed as "extend the reach that already happened organically," not "force a higher engagement rate."

**Confidence:** Medium. The reach multiple is strong and sustained (12 days post-publish, metrics still accumulating). The caveat is that engagement depth is average, so paid should prioritize video-view and brand-lift objectives over direct-response KPIs on day one. If pixel ROAS is weak in the first 7 days, rotate to a brand-awareness objective rather than killing the creative.
