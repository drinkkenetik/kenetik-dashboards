## Hypothesis

A true pre-cancel intercept is not possible in Klaviyo — Klaviyo's Subscription Cancelled trigger only fires AFTER the cancel is committed (that is what CS-197/S4ng5R covers, post-cancel). The only surface that can intervene at the moment of cancel intent is Recharge's native in-account cancellation-prevention flow shown in the customer account dashboard. Hypothesis: presenting pause / skip-shipment / swap / single-order-discount options on the Recharge cancel path — branched on the now-standardized cancellation reasons — diverts a measurable share of active-churn events before they convert to a cancellation, recovering subs that the post-cancel winback structurally cannot reach. Falsify against 30d voluntary churn baseline.

## Resolution Path (Diagnose / Remove / Refine / Add)

- **Diagnose:** The Klaviyo Canceled Subscription flow (S4ng5R, CS-197) is structurally post-cancel — it can only message a subscriber once the cancellation is already committed in Recharge. There is no Klaviyo trigger or branch that fires *during* the cancel action, so the single highest-intent moment (the customer clicking "cancel" in their account dashboard) currently has zero retention touch. The only system that owns that moment is Recharge's native cancellation flow.
- **Remove:** Nothing to remove — this is an absent intercept, not an interfering mechanism. Complementary to CS-197 (post-cancel) and CS-2026-149 (cancellation-reasons RCA); does not overlap either.
- **Refine:** Configure Recharge's built-in cancellation-prevention / retention flow in the customer account dashboard so the cancel path presents save options before the final confirmation: pause subscription, skip next shipment, change delivery frequency, swap product, and/or a single-order discount. Branch the offer on the standardized cancellation reasons (cannot afford → discount/cheaper cadence; too much product → skip / frequency change; didn't mean to start → manage/one-time purchase; no longer using → benefit reminder; taste → flavor swap).
- **Add:** Only the in-dashboard intercept step + reason-branched offer logic. Justified because no pre-cancel touch exists anywhere in the funnel and Klaviyo cannot provide one; Recharge is the only surface that can.

### What was actually built

**1. Deflection page (new step placed in front of the cancellation survey) on all three order-count branches (Orders 3+, Orders 1–3, Else):**
- Offer = **Delay and change frequency**
- Delay options = **2 weeks / 1 month / 2 months**
- Change frequency type = **Customer can choose any frequency**
- The deflection landing page is a **shared template** across branches (a single edit applies to all three).

**2. Reward-loss panels on the cancellation-survey landing pages (these are per-branch templates):** credits confirmed forfeited on cancel, so the loss framing is accurate, not a scare tactic.
- Free-gift reminder panel — dynamic `{product_title}`, shows the free gift riding on the customer's next order.
- Credit-balance panel — dynamic `{available_balance}`, with credit-application enabled and an **"Apply my credits, keep my subscription"** action.
- Reason-branched survey offers retained per tier: Orders 3+ = 25% / Delay & change frequency / Swap & discount / 20%; lower tiers = 5% / Delay / Swap / No offer.

**3. Brand-voice copy pass (Warm retention tone) across deflection + survey landing pages:**
- Removed the em dash from the deflection headline (AI-drafting tell, hard ban in customer copy). New headline names the over-supply moment: "Stacking up faster than you can drink it?"; subhead reframes cancel → adjust and reassures that subscriber pricing stays the same.
- Survey landing rewritten: removed three exclamation marks and hedge-stacking; ownership-framed credit headline ("You have {available_balance} in credits to use"); free-gift copy "It ships with your next box. Cancel now and it goes too."
- Survey intro rewritten customer-first: "Before you go, tell us what's not working, and we'll see what we can do." Added a customer-support line: **orders@drinkkenetik.com**.
- First-person CTAs throughout: "Keep my subscription", "Apply my credits, keep my subscription", "Back to my account".
- The four standardized cancellation-reason labels were left unchanged to preserve the reason taxonomy and clean measurement vs the CS-197 baseline.

### Deviations from the approved plan
- Top-level branching kept on **order count** (Orders 3+ / 1–3 / Else); the deflection lever is non-monetary (delay/frequency) and discounts stay gated in the per-tier surveys. Not reason-branched at the deflection level.
- Scope expanded beyond the approved offer mechanics: added per-branch survey-landing reward-loss panels (free gift + credit balance), a full brand-voice copy rewrite, and a support-contact line.
- Claims gate (approval flag): no cognition / "benefit reminder" branch was added, so the conditional Tier-A claims risk did not trigger; landing copy kept to convenience/flexibility.
