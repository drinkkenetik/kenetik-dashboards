## Hypothesis / Proposed Change

Audience signal contamination on `Prospecting__PMAX__Wedge A__tCPA` (campaign 23778599258) is causing PMAX's algorithm to learn against a misaligned audience pool. The "Wedge A: The Output Engineer" segment currently lists 10 finance-news interest signals (CNBC, Bloomberg, Yahoo Finance, etc.) that are unrelated to the Wedge A persona — likely a leftover from another campaign's targeting. Compounding this, all 9 Klaviyo customer lists are wired in as PMAX customer-data signals, mixing generic purchasers, retargeting audiences, and warm prospects into what should be a cold-prospecting seed. Search themes contain duplicates and weak/off-target entries.

Replacing contaminated signals with audience signals coherently aligned to the Wedge A Output Engineer persona [src: brain/kenetik-brain/references/segments-personas.md#wedge-a] will improve PMAX algorithmic learning and reduce wasted impressions. Expected impact: 20-40% CTR lift, 15-30% cold-traffic CVR lift, CPA convergence toward Wedge C PMAX parity over the 14-day relearning window.

This is a Level 1 + Level 2 intervention per the Optimization Hierarchy [src: brain/kenetik-brain/references/system-architecture.md] — primarily *remove* (cut wasted signals) with *refine* (replace with sharper coherent signals). Targeting cleanup is independent of and complementary to the LP redesign workstream now scoped for `/pages/protocol-deep-work` (separate CS to follow).

## Resolution Path (§10b)

- **Diagnose:** Funnel diagnosis 2026-05-04 identified two structural issues — (a) audience signal contamination (this CS), (b) cold-traffic LP mismatch on `/pages/protocol-deep-work` (separate workstream pending). Diagnosis surfaced from CGO direct request after observing Wedge A PMAX underperforming branded and Wedge C PMAX over recent reporting.
- **Remove:** Primary path. Cut 10 finance-news interest signals, 7 misapplied Klaviyo customer lists, and 5 weak/duplicate search themes. Removal is the single highest-leverage move because PMAX's algorithm currently weights these as positive signal.
