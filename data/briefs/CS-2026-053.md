## 1. Summary

Three-part optimization of the existing "Review request" flow (Klaviyo `Rcgah9`) based on CS-2026-036 investigation findings. Scales a proven winning email variant to 100% of traffic, adds LoyaltyLion points incentive to all messages, and fixes the SMS-to-review UX gap. Combined effort: ~3 hours. Expected to lift review submission rate from 3.7% to ≥8% and flow RPR from $0.99 to ≥$1.50.

## 2. Objective

**Hierarchy level: L3 — Customer Quality / LTV**

Reviews are the most durable form of social proof on Kenetik PDPs. Higher review volume directly improves conversion rate for all visitors, not just the reviewer. This optimization also deepens the post-purchase relationship by adding a LoyaltyLion reward hook — converting a "please do us a favor" ask into a "here's something for you" exchange.

Secondary: **L4 — Rate of Learning.** First structured test of LoyaltyLion incentive impact on review behavior. Learnings apply to all future review/UGC requests.

**SMS message (SYG3MC):**
- Append to existing copy: "Leave a review & earn 50 loyalty points → [link]"

**LoyaltyLion configuration:**
- Create new rule: "Submitted review" metric → Award 50 points
- Verify "Submitted review" metric (Klaviyo ID: `Sjxf32`) fires correctly to LoyaltyLion

### Change 3: SMS Review Link Audit & Fix (1 hour)

- Open flow `Rcgah9` in Klaviyo → navigate to SMS message (SYG3MC)
- Click the review link CTA → test on mobile device
- Document current destination URL and experience
