## Hypothesis
If D07 and D13 domain scans are updated to cross-reference Sprout Social published post counts before declaring a content drought, then false "content blackout" narratives will be eliminated and the team will receive accurate diagnoses of content performance issues vs. content volume issues.

## Background
Sessions 23-26 carried a "content blackout" narrative that was factually incorrect. Sprout Social API verification (Session 27) showed 46 posts were published in the flagged period. The synthesis and department briefs across 4 sessions propagated a false constraint — downstream decisions about creator capacity, calendar density, and content strategy were made on bad data.

Root cause: D07 (Social Performance) and D13 (Content Calendar) used calendar status fields and engagement metrics as proxies for publication counts. When engagement collapsed (IG -68%), the system inferred a blackout rather than verifying Sprout Social directly.

Evidence from L-005: "Fix: D07 and D13 should always cross-reference Sprout Social published post counts before declaring content drought."

## Proposed Change
1. Update D07 scan: Add Sprout Social published post count check as Step 1 of content volume assessment. Only declare "content drought" if published_count < threshold AND calendar shows < 3 upcoming posts.
2. Update D13 scan: Add Sprout Social published post count as a required field. Cross-reference calendar idea/confirmed status against actual published posts from same period.
3. Define drought thresholds: <5 posts/week = drought signal; 5-10 posts/week = below target; >10 posts/week = volume adequate.

## Measurement Plan
- **Baseline:** D07/D13 produce drought diagnoses based on calendar status alone
- **Target:** Next time engagement metrics drop, drought narrative is verified against Sprout before being included in synthesis
- **Measurement window:** First session where content engagement decline occurs post-implementation
- **Primary KPI:** False drought narrative = 0

## Expected Impact
- No direct revenue impact
- Prevents 4-session carry of false narratives costing team decision quality
- Unlocks accurate content performance RCA (distinguishes volume vs. quality)
