## Hypothesis
Flip the PDP buy-box default from one-time to subscription-first presentation (subscription option rendered above one-time, selected by default) to test whether anchoring to the higher-value option and simplifying the choice architecture lifts add-to-cart rate on pdp-atc.

**Element changing:** buy-box default state + option ordering in `snippets/buy-buttons.liquid` (to be written by orchestrator at step 2 of launch sequence).

**Data rationale:** Hypothesis #4 in the pdp-atc build-spec backlog. Subscription-first is a known LTV lever in CPG; its compound effect on ATC rate is untested on Kenetik's audience. This cycle measures whether the framing flip is net-neutral, accretive, or cannibalistic on ATC.

**Expected direction:** ATC rate up (+).

**Disproof:** ATC rate flat or down at ≥95% Bayesian confidence over the 7-day measurement window, OR subscription attach rate drops with no net ATC lift (pure cannibalization pattern — tells us subscription-first looks better on the surface but isn't a true decision-architecture win).
