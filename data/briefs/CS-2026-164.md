### Preview text (paired with subject variants)
  1. Men's Health Month starts today. So does your edge.
  2. Cognitive fuel for the long days ahead.
  3. Backed by a 31-day RCT in trained runners.

### Body direction
Open with MH Month framing (broad, not gendered-exclusive — frame as 'health performance' moment). Hero: clean overhead shot of a Kenetik can on a desk + morning light. Body: (1) summer demands more — longer days, more output, more competing claims on attention; (2) the brain is the foundation, not a perk — D-BHB feeds the brain through a path glucose can't; (3) here's what the research shows. Three wedge tag CTAs in the body. Footer with Korsak citation + Frontiers meta as the science block.

### Science references
- **Korsak 2026 (PMID 41773904)** — 31-day KME supplementation significantly improved cognitive function (Stroop test, p=0.015) in trained runners — cognitive gains occurred independently of running performance changes, supporting ketones as a brain-specific fuel. [src: brain/kenetik-brain/references/claims-compliance.md#focus-cognition-tier-a]
- **Frontiers in Nutrition 2026 meta-analysis** — Pooled meta-analysis of RCTs found a significant positive effect of exogenous ketones on cognitive performance (SMD = 0.26, 95% CI: 0.11–0.40, p = 0.0007) across healthy adults and neuropsychiatric populations. [src: brain/kenetik-brain/references/claims-compliance.md#meta-analyses]
