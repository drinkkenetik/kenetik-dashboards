## Hypothesis

Mobile website performance or SERP ranking degradation is causing a significant traffic loss that compounds the DTC revenue decline. If we identify and resolve the root cause, we can recover ~200-300 weekly clicks and downstream conversions.

## Verdict: HYPOTHESIS NOT SUPPORTED

The conversion rate decline is **not mobile-specific**. It is a universal demand-side decline driven by post-promotional cliff (Leadville Sweepstakes + Brain Awareness Week 2026) and reduced brand awareness from content drought.

## Original Evidence (at time of creation)

- **D09 (Session 15):** Mobile clicks dropped 32.1% WoW (2,081 → 1,413). Mobile represents 73% of total traffic.
- **D09:** Brand query "kenetik" clicks down 25.5% (428 → 319). CTR dropped from 20.2% → 15.9%.
- **D09:** Keyword degradation across top queries: "kenetik energy drink" -26.4%, "kinetic energy drink" -58.2%.
- **D09:** Desktop traffic stable (-5.9%), isolating the issue to mobile.
- **D04:** Paid Search ROAS breakout (8.88x, +87%) rules out market-wide demand collapse — organic-specific issue.
- **D01:** Concurrent DTC order volume decline (-55% adjusted) suggests traffic loss is material to revenue.

## Investigation Scope

1. **Core Web Vitals audit:** Run PageSpeed Insights / Lighthouse on drinkkenetik.com mobile. Check LCP, FID, CLS.
2. **SERP position audit:** Check average position change for top 20 keywords. Identify if Google algorithm update or competitor rank surge.

5. **Cross-reference with D04:** Confirm paid search traffic is stable (organic-specific issue).

## Investigation Findings (April 1, 2026)

### Finding 1: CVR Decline Is Universal, Not Mobile-Specific

Comparing Mar 16–Apr 1 vs Feb 27–Mar 15 (Leadville Sweepstakes + Brain Awareness Week 2026 period):

| Device | Sessions (Current) | CVR (Current) | CVR (Prior) | Change |
|--------|-------------------|---------------|-------------|--------|
| Mobile | 21,985 | 3.41% | 14.44% | -76.4% |
| Desktop | 4,649 | 4.15% | 14.92% | -72.2% |
| Tablet | 318 | 5.35% | 14.75% | -63.7% |

Near-identical proportional decline across all devices rules out mobile-specific technical cause.

### Finding 2: Post-Promotional Demand Cliff Is Primary Driver

Direct traffic collapsed from 12,090 → 3,272 sessions (-72.9%) and CVR dropped from 30.88% → 5.23%. This single channel accounts for ~3,562 lost transactions. Prior-period Direct CVR was artificially elevated by Leadville Sweepstakes and Brain Awareness Week 2026 promotions.

| Channel | Sessions (Cur) | Sessions (Prior) | CVR (Cur) | CVR (Prior) |
|---------|---------------|-----------------|-----------|-------------|
| Direct | 3,272 | 12,090 | 5.23% | 30.88% |
| Google Ads | 9,013 | 10,615 | 4.04% | 6.90% |
| Facebook Ads | 8,494 | 3,994 | 1.08% | 0.90% |
| Google Organic | 2,605 | 4,134 | 2.46% | 5.25% |
| Klaviyo | 1,474 | 1,293 | 12.82% | 10.05% |

### Finding 3: Brand Search Volume Decline Correlates with Content Drought

GSC mobile impressions declined 42%. Misspelling/discovery variants dropped hardest:
- "kenetik" (core brand query): 300 clicks vs. 516 prior (-41.9%)
- "kinetic energy drink": 73 vs. 226 (-67.7%)
- "kinetik energy drink": 33 vs. 103 (-68.0%)
- "kenetik energy drink": 81 vs. 154 (-47.4%)

Impression volume declining = fewer people searching, not ranking position loss.

### Finding 4: FB Ads Scaling with Low CVR

Facebook Ads sessions +113% (Unbound Sweep ramp) but CVR only 1.08%. Volume masking traffic decline but not generating revenue.

### Finding 5: Core Web Vitals (Inconclusive)

PageSpeed Insights API inaccessible. Manual check recommended for Cliff. Given universal cross-device decline, CWV degradation extremely unlikely to be contributing.
