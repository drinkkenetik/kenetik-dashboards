## Problem Statement

Three signals are converging that create a positioning window:

1. **Cultural signal**: "Brain fuel" is trending across Google Trends, Reddit (r/nootropics, r/biohacking), and wellness media as a category descriptor for cognitive performance products.

2. **Science signal**: A new RCT on exogenous ketones and cognitive function was published in the last 7 days, providing fresh, peer-reviewed evidence that directly supports Kenetik's cognitive performance positioning.

3. **Competitive signal**: KEY Energy Drink ($4M seed, Coca-Cola/PepsiCo founders) is entering the functional energy space with cognitive performance messaging. Multiple new entrants in the BHB supplement category.

The window is time-limited. First mover on science-backed "brain fuel" messaging wins the narrative. If Kenetik doesn't claim this positioning, competitors will.

## Proposed Solution

**Week 1 (Mar 10–17) — Content Sprint**:

1. **Science Distillation**: Matt runs /generate-science-distillation on the new RCT. Output: 1 blog post, 1 email module, 3 social-ready data points.

2. **Social Content**: Charlotte creates 3–5 pieces of organic social content around "brain fuel" positioning:
   - Infographic: "What happens to your brain on ketones" (data from RCT)
   - Short-form video concept: "Your brain's preferred fuel" (educational)
   - Community engagement post: Reddit cross-post to r/nootropics

3. **Web/SEO**: Nick coordinates SEO-optimized blog post targeting "brain fuel" and related keywords. Anchor content for organic search capture.

4. **Email**: Include science distillation module in next scheduled campaign. Also feeds into CS-2026-007 Touch 2 (Cleetus cohort education email).

**Week 2 (Mar 17–24) — Amplification**:

5. **Paid amplification**: If organic engagement rate exceeds 3%, Katie considers boosting top-performing social content.

6. **Creator seeding**: Lawrence briefs 2–3 creators on the "brain fuel" angle with the science data points for authentic integration.
