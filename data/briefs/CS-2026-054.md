## Problem Statement

91.6% of enrolled creators (229/250) are dormant — they enrolled but never activated. The Feb-Mar 2026 enrollment cohort has near-zero activation because there is no structured onboarding sequence post-enrollment. Revenue is concentrated in 2 active creators (BSC + BRAD = 15.8% of total creator revenue). This is a structural onboarding gap, not a motivation problem.

**Evidence:**
- D08: 229/250 enrolled creators inactive (91.6% dormancy)
- D08: 21 active discount codes in 7d, revenue +27% WoW ($3,059→$3,880) but concentrated in 2 people
- Content dept brief: No activation support post-enrollment pathway exists
- pat-011 [src: data/performance-memory/cross-surface-pm.json#pat-011]: Creator program dormancy flagged 6+ consecutive sessions

## Hypothesis

A structured 14-day onboarding activation sequence (brief→ideas→feedback→first post) will achieve 30-50% activation rate for the Feb-Mar dormant cohort, reducing concentration risk and establishing a repeatable creator activation funnel.

## Measurement Plan

- **Primary metric:** Activation rate (first post within 14 days of sequence start)
- **Baseline:** 8.4% activation rate (21/250 currently active)
- **Target:** 30-50% activation rate for targeted cohort
- **Measurement window:** 30 days from first cohort entry
- **Secondary metrics:** Time to first post, content quality score, creator revenue per activated creator
