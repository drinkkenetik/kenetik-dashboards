## Learning Notes

**Measurement completed: 2026-04-08 (Day 7) — Outcome: Underperformed (with benchmarking caveat)**

Science-led April Fools email ("We'd never joke about your brain" / Machado 2022 qEEG study) sent to 17,128 recipients (not the projected ~51,643 — standard suppression segments reduced audience by 67%).

**Performance vs. original targets:**
- Revenue: $1,183.61 vs $6,000 target (80% miss)
- Open rate: 46.5% vs 47% target (barely missed)
- Click rate: 0.71% vs "8% CTR" target (target was misset)
- CTOR: 1.52% (not in original targets — should have been the engagement KPI)
- Unsubscribe rate: 0.21% vs <0.3% target (clean)

**Performance vs. correct benchmarks (7 comparable non-promo content emails, Mar-Apr 2026):**
- Open rate: 46.5% = **#1 in set** (avg 41.5%)
- Click rate: 0.71% = **#1 in set** (avg 0.64%)
- CTOR: 1.52% = **Top 3** (avg 1.56%)
- Revenue: $1,184 = mid-range (avg $1,908 for revenue-generating sends; range $874–$2,123)

**Key insight:** The $6K revenue target and 8% "CTR" were promotional-campaign benchmarks incorrectly applied to a non-promotional science engagement email. Promotional emails with offers (e.g., DST Flash Sale 20% off) achieve $7K+ revenue and 1.1%+ click rates because they have a commercial hook. Content/engagement emails to ~17K audiences consistently generate $874–$2,123 revenue and 0.5–0.7% click rates. Future non-promotional science emails should be benchmarked against content-email baselines, not promo baselines.

**What worked:** Science subject line generated best-in-class opens. The credibility hook ("We'd never joke about your brain" + Machado 2022 citation in preview text) drove curiosity effectively.

**What to improve:** For future content emails, expected revenue should be set at $1,000–$2,000 for ~17K sends. If revenue ≥$6K is the goal, the email needs a promotional offer paired with the science content.
