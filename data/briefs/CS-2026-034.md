## Hypothesis

The ambassador program has structurally collapsed (90% inactive, revenue -55% WoW) because of a lack of active engagement management, unclear posting expectations, and no tiered incentive structure. A formal re-engagement program with tier stratification will reactivate 20-30% of the roster within 14 days and stabilize creator-driven revenue.

- **D08:** Top creator CLEETUS at $858 (down from $1,200+ baseline). All top 5 showing reduced cadence.

## Implementation Plan

### Phase 1: Audit & Stratification (Mar 21-24)
1. Pull full Superfiliate roster with 90-day revenue data
2. Stratify into tiers:
   - **Tier 1 (>$500/mo):** High-touch management, custom briefs, priority product access
   - **Tier 2 ($100-500/mo):** Monthly check-ins, content calendar alignment
   - **Tier 3 (<$100/mo):** Automated engagement, quarterly review
   - **Inactive ($0/90 days):** Re-engagement outreach or sunsetting
3. Contact 50 creators with $0 revenue this month to diagnose blocker

### Phase 2: Re-Engagement Sprint (Mar 25-28)
1. Personal outreach to Tier 1 + Tier 2 creators (direct message or call)
2. Provide Q2 content calendar with weekly themes
3. Ship product kits to top 10 dormant creators
4. Set clear cadence expectations: Tier 1 = 3 posts/week, Tier 2 = 1 post/week

### Phase 3: Measurement (Mar 28 - Apr 11)
1. Track posting cadence per tier
2. Track revenue attribution per tier
3. Track null discount code percentage
4. Decision point: scale successful tiers, sunset non-responsive creators
