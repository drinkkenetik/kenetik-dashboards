## Problem Statement

The Cleetus McFarland activation (Mar 7–9) acquired ~4,700 new customers in 3 days. These customers entered through a deep-discount promo (AOV collapsed from $50.33 to $12.53) and are currently hitting the generic Welcome Series flow — which has a 3.82% unsub rate and $0.06 RPR on its highest-volume message.

Without a tailored retention playbook, most of these customers will:
1. Receive generic Welcome emails that don't acknowledge HOW they found Kenetik
2. Unsubscribe at 3.82% per email (the General flow rate)
3. Never convert to full-price subscribers
4. Represent a negative-margin acquisition with no LTV recovery

## Proposed Solution

Create a dedicated Cleetus Cohort nurture sequence that:

**Touch 1 (Day 3 post-purchase) — Plain text email**: "Your Kenetik pack just landed (here's what to expect)" — Sent from cleetus@DrinkKenetik.com, signed by Cleetus. Acknowledges the Cleetus connection, introduces the brand story ("metabolic brain fuel"), sets expectations for the experience ("calm, clear focus that settles in about 20–30 minutes"), and provides a simple first-week protocol (drink one can morning/early afternoon → give it 20–30 min → notice what changes). Warm, not salesy. Plain text for personal feel.

**Touch 2 (Day 7) — Designed email**: "Your Brain on Kenetik" — Education-focused. Explains dual-fuel brain model (glucose + ketones), introduces bioidentical D-BHB mechanism, positions Kenetik as the clean alternative to caffeine via a side-by-side comparison table (D-BHB ketones vs stimulant, calm clarity vs spike + jitters, 4–6 hour duration vs 2–4 hour crash, no side effects vs jitters/anxiety/sleep disruption, no tolerance vs tolerance develops quickly). Includes customer testimonial and product badges (zero caffeine, zero sugar, Informed Sport). CTA: "Fuel Your Brain" / "Shop Kenetik."

**Touch 3 (Day 10) — Designed email + SMS**: "Introducing the Matt Hughes Brain Health Journey" — Repositioned from the original brief to capitalize on the Matt Hughes documentary as the narrative vehicle for the "brain fuel" science message. Leads with Matt Hughes (former UFC Champion) and his brain health journey with Kenetik, then layers three research-backed proof points as headline stats: +30% cerebral blood flow (Svart 2018), +18% alpha brain activity (Machado 2022, Kenetik's own clinical study), and improved processing speed via DSST (Walsh 2021). Includes references section with full citations. Customer testimonial from cognitive performance use case. CTA: "Fuel Your Brain" / "Continue Your Protocol." Paired SMS drives to the documentary and reinforces the email narrative.

**Touch 4 (Day 14) — Plain text email**: "Ready for round two? Here's 15% off your first order" — First conversion ask. Sent from cleetus@DrinkKenetik.com, signed by Cleetus. Introduces cyclic metabolic switching concept as the scientific frame for daily use. Bridges from the free pack to paid: "Your free pack was the introduction. This is the invitation to make it a daily tool." 15% off with code CLEETUS15. Lists all three consumer formats (Cans, Shots, Concentrate) with quick descriptors. Free shipping on orders over $100. Plain text for personal, low-pressure feel.

**Touch 5 (Day 21) — Designed email**: "Never Let Your Brain Fuel Tank Hit E" — Final conversion CTA with subscription step-up. Cleetus/NASCAR racing visual identity for cohort-specific resonance. Positions subscription as the bridge from "trying a supplement" to "having a daily edge." CLEETUS15 code stacks on evergreen 20% Subscribe & Save discount for 35% off first subscription order, then 20% off for life. Includes subscription value props (ships on your schedule, modify/cancel anytime, 3% Kenetik Credit back) and simple how-it-works (pick format → choose frequency → apply code → brain fuel on autopilot). CTA: "Subscribe & Save 20%" / "Get Your Daily Brain Fuel."

**Social reinforcement**: Nick coordinates retargeting content on social that mirrors the email narrative arc. Charlotte creates 2–3 organic posts that speak to the Cleetus audience specifically.

### 2026-03-16 — Final creative build and publish (Katie)

**Status change**: Approved → Published

**What changed from the original brief:**

1. **Touch 3 repositioned around Matt Hughes Brain Health Journey.** The original brief described Touch 3 as "capitalize on the 'brain fuel' trending topic (CS-2026-008), testimonials from cognitive performance use case." During creative development, we repositioned this touch to lead with the Matt Hughes documentary as the narrative vehicle. Matt Hughes (former UFC Champion) and his brain health journey with Kenetik gives the science message a real human story — more compelling than a generic "brain fuel" trending topic hook. The three research proof points (+30% CBF, +18% alpha activity, improved processing speed) are now framed as "what the research says is happening under the hood for you and Matt" rather than standalone stats. This creates a stronger emotional-to-rational arc: person → proof → protocol.

2. **SMS added to Touch 3.** The original brief specified email-only for Touches 1–3. Given Devon's daily pulse flagging email CTR collapse (0.52–0.83%, all campaigns below 1%) and SMS having a 46x CTR advantage, we paired Touch 3 with an SMS that drives to the Matt Hughes documentary. This hedges against the email deliverability risk for what is arguably the most important engagement touch in the sequence — if the science story lands, Touches 4–5 convert.

3. **Touch 5 pricing mechanics clarified.** The brief specified "Subscribe & Save 20%" as the Touch 5 offer. The final creative uses CLEETUS15 (the 15% code from Touch 4) stacking on the evergreen 20% S&S discount, producing 35% off the first subscription order. This is intentional — rather than issuing a new code that could create confusion or stack unpredictably with CLEETUS15, we let the existing code do double duty. The customer sees one consistent code across Touches 4–5 with increasing value as they move from one-time to subscription.

4. **Plain text for Touches 1 and 4 confirmed.** Both sent from cleetus@DrinkKenetik.com and signed by Cleetus. The alternating plain text / designed email pattern (plain → designed → designed → plain → designed) keeps the sequence from feeling like a marketing barrage and lets the Cleetus-signed touches feel personal and authentic to the audience.

5. **Touch 2 Kenetik vs Caffeine comparison table.** The side-by-side was a creative execution choice not in the original brief. It gives the "What Ketones Actually Do" education message a concrete competitive frame that this audience (many of whom are heavy caffeine users) can immediately relate to.
