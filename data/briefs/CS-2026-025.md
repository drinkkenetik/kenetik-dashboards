## Problem Statement

Kenetik's attribution stack is structurally broken. The "Undefined" channel now accounts for $16.9K–$21.5K per week (22–26% of total revenue) with zero Polar Pixel attribution. Superfiliate-tracked revenue collapsed 48.5% and 93% in orders, yet combined creator-attributed revenue held at ~$26K — indicating revenue migration from tracked to untracked channels. Polar Pixel shows impossible metrics for Mar 7–9 (AOV $3–$6 across channels). Organic Search AOV reads $20.53 vs $88.45 baseline. Data source discrepancies compound: IG reports 23 posts published while the marketing calendar shows 0/20.

Every downstream decision — budget allocation, channel ROI, LTV:CAC, creator investment, paid media optimization — is built on data that is currently unreliable.

## Hypothesis

A structured audit of UTM parameters, Polar Pixel configuration, Superfiliate tracking codes, and discount code mappings will classify >80% of Undefined channel revenue into proper attribution buckets, restoring decision-quality data for Q2 planning.
