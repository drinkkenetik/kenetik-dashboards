### Multi-dimensional assessment

- **L1 — Margin:** Below. Net loss of $233.80 vs. expected $2,400 margin. Below even the brief's bearish scenario.
- **L3 — Customer Quality:** Too early — only 3 purchasers, insufficient for cohort read.
- **L4 — Learning:** Hypothesis NOT supported. The 9.57x predecessor (CS-2026-029) was an audience × timing effect (retargeting within Brain Awareness Week), not a creative × audience effect. Athlete content reinforces existing intent; it does not generate it in cold/interest audiences.

### What happened

Scaled CS-029's winning Matt Hughes content to full $500/wk budget across prospecting + interest + retargeting. Clicks held on the re-edit (2.82% CTR), but conversion collapsed. Prospecting and interest audiences generated zero purchases despite receiving the majority of impressions. Kill trigger (<0.8x ROAS at Day 7) was hit on Apr 10.

### Action taken

Per owner (Katie): prospecting and interest adsets were paused operationally ~Apr 10 after Day 7 kill trigger. Retargeting is being retained, with additional Matt Hughes content loaded into retargeting audiences going forward. No new spawn ChangeSet — iterate is being handled as BAU paid-media operation.

### Learning (logged to cross-surface-pm.json as L-040, paid-media domain, significant_variance)

Scaled CS-029's 9.57x retargeting winner to full-budget prospecting+interest+retargeting mix → prospecting/interest produced 0 purchases and blended ROAS collapsed to 0.38x on $288 spend. **Athlete content is a retargeting reinforcement asset, not a prospecting asset.** Creative performance does not generalize across audience temperature when the predecessor win was in a warm/contextual slot. For future scale ChangeSets on paid_meta: if the predecessor's win was retargeting-only, do not assume the creative will perform in prospecting — test prospecting separately at a retargeting-adjusted budget before assuming transferability.
