**Meta-learning (for the Learning Library + graduation pipeline design):**

Cultural-moment graduations (awareness weeks, holidays, named events) have a much shorter decide-to-launch runway than evergreen graduations. The standard weekly-scan → draft → review → approve → publish pipeline is tuned for evergreen content that can sit in queue; it's too slow for moment-tied organic winners.

**Forward application:**

1. At the graduation scan step (Moe's weekly run), flag moment-tied candidates with a `cultural_window_expires:` date. Any graduation candidate whose source post references a named week/holiday/event should carry this field.
2. In the graduation queue UI, sort by `cultural_window_expires` ascending for moment-tied items — they get pulled forward regardless of scan date.
3. Cultural-moment candidates should route to review within 48h of scan flag, not sit in the weekly batch. If the moment has <7 days left at scan time, either fast-track or auto-close as expired.
4. Revisit the 11-day scan latency (post 2026-03-19 → flagged 2026-03-30). Moe's weekly cadence means up to 6 days of baseline latency on any candidate; for cultural-moment posts, consider a daily "moment-tied" scan that fires within 24h of publish for any post carrying holiday/awareness/event tags.

**Reusable learnings:** JustJosephD as a creator partner generated 3.9x reach on this collab. The creator relationship is durable even though this particular graduation didn't ship — worth considering for future evergreen mental-performance content without the BAW tie.
