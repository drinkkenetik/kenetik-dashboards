## Problem

D09 mobile bounce jumped **+5.5pp WoW to 64.3%** while desktop held flat. Mobile is **79% of site traffic**, so this single metric is gating nearly every paid + organic CVR story on the site.

Creator landing pages are the worst offenders and are actively absorbing paid spend:

| LP | CVR | Bounce |
|---|---|---|
| /pages/ashton | 0.24% | 92% |
| /pages/roman | 1.34% | 72% |
| /pages/cleetus | 2.64% | 81% |

These are the exact LPs receiving Meta + creator-driven traffic. Paid-media waste reduction (CS-2026-053) is worthless if fresh creative keeps routing into broken destinations.

## Scope

Two-phase investigation:

**Phase 1 — Diagnose (due 2026-04-15)**
- GA4 landing-page × source × device segmentation to isolate which creative/channel combinations are driving the mobile bounce spike.
- Lighthouse mobile perf audit across homepage + all creator LPs (LCP, CLS, TBT).
- Layout-shift / CTA-visibility audit on creator LPs specifically.

**Phase 2 — Test (due 2026-04-22)**
- A/B test: creator LP (control) vs. redirect-to-homepage-with-attribution (variant) for paid traffic from each creator.
- Parallel: redesign Ashton LP (worst offender) — above-the-fold CTA, mobile-first layout.

## Team

## Success criteria

- Root cause named for mobile bounce WoW delta
- Mobile bounce rate **<60%** site-wide (from 64.3%)
- Creator LP CVR **≥2.5%** across all three (from 0.24–2.64%)
- Decision by Apr 22: keep / redesign / kill each creator LP
