## Hypothesis

Subscription churn is structurally accelerating (May 14.6% vs Apr 12.7%; active subs 2,380, -41/7d). The Canceled Subscription flow (S4ng5R) shows 33% open / 0.62% CTR / 1 conversion from 161 recipients — subscribers are opening (intent to engage) but there is no retention hook at the moment of peak cancel intent. Hypothesis: inserting a pre-cancel offer intercept (pause/skip option or single-order discount) BEFORE the final cancel confirmation captures a measurable share of active-churn events. This is a distinct funnel point from CS-2026-186 winback (post-cancel) — it intercepts pre-cancel. Falsify against 30d voluntary churn (May 309 events) to confirm whether a pre-cancel intercept materially reduces voluntary churn and supports active_sub_count recovery toward the 2,800 target.

## Resolution Path (Diagnose / Remove / Refine / Add)

- **Diagnose:** The Canceled Subscription flow (S4ng5R) fires AFTER a subscriber has already confirmed cancellation. It earns a 33% open rate (intent to engage is present) but converts 0.62% (1/161) — because by the time the email lands, the cancel is done and the only ask is a cold re-subscribe. The structural gap is that Kenetik has no retention touch at the single highest-intent moment: the cancel-confirmation step itself.
- **Remove:** Nothing to remove — the gap is an absent intercept, not an interfering mechanism. (Confirmed not a duplicate: CS-2026-186 is winback/post-cancel; CS-2026-149 is a cancellation-reasons RCA. Neither places an offer pre-confirmation.)
- **Refine:** First preference — refine the EXISTING cancel funnel rather than build net-new assets: insert a pause/skip-shipment option and/or a single-order discount on the cancel-confirmation screen and as the first email of S4ng5R (before the post-cancel content), reusing existing Klaviyo offer mechanics. This is a refine, not a from-scratch flow.
- **Add:** Only the intercept step itself (one Klaviyo flow-branch + one offer block). Justified because no pre-cancel touch exists anywhere in the funnel and churn is structurally accelerating (May 14.6% vs Apr 12.7%); the marginal asset is the cheapest containable lever against a -41/7d active-sub trajectory.
