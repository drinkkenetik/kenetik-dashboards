## Hypothesis

Subscription churn is structurally accelerating (May 14.6% vs Apr 12.7%; active subs 2,380, -41/7d). The Canceled Subscription flow (S4ng5R) shows 33% open / 0.62% CTR / 1 conversion from 161 recipients — subscribers are opening (intent to engage) but there is no retention hook at the moment of peak cancel intent. Hypothesis: inserting a pre-cancel offer intercept (pause/skip option or single-order discount) BEFORE the final cancel confirmation captures a measurable share of active-churn events. This is a distinct funnel point from CS-2026-186 winback (post-cancel) — it intercepts pre-cancel. Falsify against 30d voluntary churn (May 309 events) to confirm whether a pre-cancel intercept materially reduces voluntary churn and supports active_sub_count recovery toward the 2,800 target.

## Resolution Path (Diagnose / Remove / Refine / Add)

- **Diagnose:** The Canceled Subscription flow (S4ng5R) fires AFTER a subscriber has already confirmed cancellation. It earns a 33% open rate (intent to engage is present) but converts 0.62% (1/161) — because by the time the email lands, the cancel is done and the only ask is a cold re-subscribe. The structural gap is that Kenetik has no retention touch at the single highest-intent moment: the cancel-confirmation step itself.
- **Remove:** Nothing to remove — the gap is an absent intercept, not an interfering mechanism. (Confirmed not a duplicate: CS-2026-186 is winback/post-cancel; CS-2026-149 is a cancellation-reasons RCA. Neither places an offer pre-confirmation.)
- **Refine:** First preference — refine the EXISTING cancel funnel rather than build net-new assets: insert a pause/skip-shipment option and/or a single-order discount on the cancel-confirmation screen and as the first email of S4ng5R (before the post-cancel content), reusing existing Klaviyo offer mechanics. This is a refine, not a from-scratch flow.
- **Add:** Only the intercept step itself (one Klaviyo flow-branch + one offer block). Justified because no pre-cancel touch exists anywhere in the funnel and churn is structurally accelerating (May 14.6% vs Apr 12.7%); the marginal asset is the cheapest containable lever against a -41/7d active-sub trajectory.

## §10d — As-Built Payload (GO-LIVE 2026-06-12)

**Go-live confirmed:** 2026-06-12 AM — Canceled Subscription flow **S4ng5R** verified live in Klaviyo by Katie Spaller. draft→published; executed/published 2026-06-12; 30d measurement window (measurement_due 2026-07-12).

**As-built note:** What shipped is the **post-cancel, reason-branched winback** build of S4ng5R (per the approved flow map). The flow map is the canonical as-built spec. The *pre-cancel intercept* originally scoped here is not possible in Klaviyo (Klaviyo fires only after cancellation) — that work moved to **CS-2026-200** (Recharge in-account cancellation-prevention flow). Cancellation-reason values are already standardized, so branch logic maps cleanly.

**Flow structure (as built):**
- **Trigger:** Subscription Cancelled on recharge
- **Flow filters:** Started subscription 0× since starting this flow; Recharge subscription reactivated 0× since starting this flow
- **2-min delay → Subscription Cancelled Email #1:** plain-text cancellation confirmation, sent from customer-experience (not founder). (Open Q: dedupe vs Recharge's own cancel-confirmation email.)
- **Branch by cancellation reason** — Katie's Klaviyo note: many reasons exist; flow splits on the most-recurring set:
  - **Cannot afford / too expensive** → 45-day wait → *Cannot afford product* Email #1 (value / per-serving framing; no deep one-off discount — lean toward a cheaper subscription option / "we believe everyone should have ketones").
  - **Didn't mean to start a subscription** → 10-day wait → *Accidental Subscription* Email #1 (plain text, customer-experience; how to make a one-time purchase via the portal; surface savings/benefits of subscribing).
  - **Have too much product** → 25-day wait → *Too much product* Email #1, then Email #2 (educate on pause/skip + delivery-frequency change every 30/14 days; portal self-serve; free gifts/exclusive discounts + up to 100% credit-back on repeat purchases).
  - **No longer want to use the product** → *No longer using* Email #1 (from founder Devon; long-term ketone benefits tied to a goal — Deep Work / Focus / Stay Sharp; open reply).
  - **Don't like the taste** → *Taste* Email #1 (customer-experience taste expert; flavor feedback + flavor variety), then Email #2 (designed; 20% off for life on restart; ideal delivery 30/14 days).
