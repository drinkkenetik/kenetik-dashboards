## Problem
**D13 has been RED for 6 consecutive sessions**, triggering the Step 8a structural escalation. The pattern is not a staffing tactics issue; it's an infrastructure issue masquerading as a content problem.

Measurement from D13 scan:
- **Marketing calendar fill:** 34/73 populated (47%) — 39 entries marked `needs_planning`
- **Content calendar queue:** 9/9 entries stuck at `idea` within the 7-day publish window (zero forward production)
- **Product-in-context coverage:** 0% vs 25% category target (zero SKU-visible content shipped)
- **Katie concentration:** 14/25 non-draft CSes (56%) concentrated on one owner with 4 sequential send-slip events

Previous attempts to fix D13 with calendar-level sprints (CS-2026-057 Sprout verification, CS-2026-075 emergency calendar sprint) have repeatedly produced short-term lift followed by reversion. The underlying constraint is **production capacity** — we have demand (briefs sitting in queue), routing (the flywheel works), and measurement — what we lack is shippable output per person per day on the creative-production leg.

## Approach

### Phase 1: Baseline production capacity (Week 1)
1. **Owner-surface contract table:** Charlotte, Nicole, Katie each commit to a per-surface publish rate (per week) — e.g., Charlotte: 3 IG reels + 2 TikToks + 1 blog/week; Nicole: 2 email sends + 1 SMS/week; Katie: 3 graduation-to-paid + 2 creator briefs/week
2. **Production queue SLA:** Each brief in the queue gets a per-stage SLA (brief→draft 48h, draft→review 24h, review→publish 24h) with escalation to Devon if a brief is stuck >72h at any stage
3. **Owner-concentration circuit-breaker:** If any single owner > 40% of active CSes, auto-flag for CGO review and require either reassignment or explicit Devon override

### Phase 2: Infrastructure (Week 2-3)
4. **AI-assisted first-draft pipeline:** Each brief lands with a Claude-generated first-draft across all target surfaces (copy variants, caption, hook options); owner edits and ships rather than creates from scratch
5. **Template library:** Top 5 highest-performing formats per surface become templates with slot-based fill-in (hook, proof point, CTA) to reduce cognitive load per brief
6. **Daily ship standup (15 min):** Asynchronous standup where each owner posts: what shipped yesterday / what's blocking today / what ships today

### Phase 3: Measurement (Week 4+)
7. **D13 score target:** ≥60 (GREEN) for 4 consecutive sessions before declaring structural fix successful
8. **Production queue SLA adherence:** ≥85% of briefs hit per-stage SLA
9. **Owner concentration:** No single owner >35% of active CSes
