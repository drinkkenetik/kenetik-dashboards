## Problem

Three domains converge on the same story — the Feb 2026 acquisition cohort is retaining worse than any cohort in the prior six months:

- **D01:** Feb M1 repeat rate **12.8%** vs 6-month avg **~19%** (−33% YoY). DTC revenue −7.4% WoW and new customer rate −21.4 ppts already showing downstream.
- **D09:** New session volume −16.3% WoW with mobile bounce +5.5pp. Acquisition traffic is both smaller and lower-quality.
- **D04:** Prospecting softness — Meta MER 1.32x (below 2.0x L1 guardrail); Ashton 0.25x ROAS, PMAX 0.36x. Low-ROAS spend disproportionately acquired Feb cohort.

Working hypothesis: the Feb mix (Unbound Sweep traffic + ARCA activation + creator-LP paid spend) pulled in a structurally lower-retention customer than our baseline. If true, current Meta/PMAX cuts address symptoms but not the channel-cohort quality problem.

## Scope

1. **Segment Feb 2026 cohort by acquisition source** — channel × campaign × LP. Compute M1 repeat, LTV:CAC, AOV, and repeat spend by segment.
2. **Compare to 6-month and YoY baselines** at same granularity.
3. **Identify the offenders** — which acquisition source(s) account for the M1 gap? Isolate Unbound, ARCA, specific creator LPs, and broad Meta prospecting.
4. **Decide the lever** — suppress the channel(s), build source-specific onboarding/post-purchase flow, or accept degraded economics for volume.
