## §10a Hypothesis

The 2026 Hodie Bash (45-year invite-only charity gala benefiting Lurie Children's Hospital; 2026 edition is July 10 at Metro Chicago, headlined by Collective Soul) includes Kenetik in its VIP bags with QR-coded print materials. Those QR scans currently have no purpose-built destination. Routing high-intent, affluent event scans to the homepage wastes the warmest moment of intent (bag in hand, brand just introduced).

**Hypothesis:** A dedicated co-branded VIP landing page -- built entirely from existing `KS-partner-*` sections, re-skinned for the Wedge B (Ageless Lifestyle / Stimulant Refugee) VIP audience, with an exclusive code (HODIE25, 25% off first order) and email capture -- will convert VIP-bag QR scans into first orders and captured emails materially better than a homepage redirect. Target: `lp_level_cvr_new_visitors` at or above site-average new-visitor CVR within the 14-day post-event window, plus incremental `new_customer_count` from the event cohort.

## §10b Resolution Path (Diagnose / Remove / Refine / Add)

- **Diagnose:** No dedicated destination exists for Hodie Bash QR traffic; scans would land on a generic surface with no event context, no offer, and no capture path. The KS-partner-* section family already powers high-quality co-branded landers (e.g. CS-2026-138 partner LP work).
- **Remove:** N/A -- net-new surface.
- **Refine:** Clone the existing `page.partner-landing.json` template (full KS-partner-* section stack) and re-skin copy for the Hodie Bash VIP audience. Removed the celebrity-testimonial section (`KS-partner-testimonial`) since there is no named partner -- avoids a fabricated VIP quote and keeps the page honest. All other sections reused as-is.
- **Add:** No new Liquid. The only true "add" is one new template JSON assembled from existing sections -- Resolution Hierarchy Step 4 (Add) avoided at the code layer.
