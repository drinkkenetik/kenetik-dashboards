## Measurement Plan — Delegated Model

This codification CS does NOT carry its own measurement window. The codification is the deliverable; its value is realized when the template is deployed on a real GP-class drop. Forcing a 14d window on this CS would either nag for measurement of a doc commit (meaningless) or auto-close as `force_closed_no_measurement` after 90d if no GP-class drop fires inside the window — neither outcome reflects reality.

**Delegated measurement model:**

1. Each future deployment of the L-087 template is its own ChangeSet (`/new-changeset` with a reference to `brain/kenetik-brain/references/lifecycle-playbook.md#L-087`).
2. That deployment CS declares expected metrics against the acceptable bands defined in the playbook section (Email CVR ≥10%, RPR ≥$7.50, click ≥18%, open ≥45%; SMS conv ≥9%, RPR ≥$6.50, unsub <0.85%).
3. The deployment CS's measurement window (14d post-send by default for Klaviyo) IS the measurement of the L-087 mechanic at that firing.
4. Outcome routing:
   - **In-band landing** → adds an instance to the playbook section's change log (Instance #3, #4, …) and strengthens the confirmed status.
   - **Out-of-band miss** → triggers `/rca` against the failed instance and a revision ChangeSet that edits `lifecycle-playbook.md#L-087` directly.

**Reference baseline (kept here for the deployment CSes to cite):** Instance #1/#2 — 12.91% CVR / $9.97 RPR / 23.1% click / 53.9% open on the email segment (1,761 recipients); 11.3% conv / $8.41 RPR on the SMS pair (541 recipients); SMS unsub 0.37% (well within 0.85% guardrail). Mature-attribution conversion value $17,531. [src: `cgo-logs/department-briefs/2026-05-19-s1/dept-lifecycle-marketing.md` SIGNALS §D02-1]

## Rollback Plan

Operationally simple — three reverts:

1. `git revert` the codification commit to remove the playbook file.
2. Revert the L-087 PM status to `active` (candidate).
3. Tracker: set status `published` → `rolled_back`, populate `rolled_back_date` and `rolled_back_reason`.

Trigger conditions for rollback: subsequent deployment of the template lands materially outside the acceptable bands AND `/rca` traces the miss to a flaw in the codified spec (not external conditions). For a doc-only CS this is a very low-likelihood path; the more common revision pathway is a new ChangeSet that edits the playbook section rather than reverting it.
