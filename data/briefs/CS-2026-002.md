## 1. Summary

Build two new Klaviyo segments to complement the existing "UE | Cleetus Audience" (Yqj4WM) segment: a broader all-subscribers segment (not just purchasers) and a 30-day conversion tracking segment. Together these three segments enable Welcome Series routing (CS-2026-001), engagement metric isolation, and cohort performance tracking.

## 2. Objective

**Optimization Hierarchy Level 4 — Rate of Learning**

Without proper segmentation, the Cleetus cohort dilutes every engagement metric in Klaviyo — open rates, click rates, deliverability scores. This makes it impossible to learn what's working for organic subscribers vs. influencer-acquired subscribers. Segmentation is the prerequisite for every other intervention on this cohort.

## 7. Payload — After

**New Segment A: "Influencer-Acquired — Cleetus All Subscribers"**

Klaviyo filter criteria:
- Profile created date = on or after 2026-03-07
- AND one or more of:
  - `$source` contains "superfiliate" OR "Superfiliate"
  - OR Discount code used contains "CLEETUS" or "CLETUS" or "cleetus" or "cletus"
  - OR Initial referral URL contains "cleetus"
- Note: Does NOT require Placed Order (captures non-purchasers too)

Purpose: Full-cohort coverage for Welcome Series routing (CS-2026-001), engagement scoring isolation, campaign targeting.

**New Segment B: "Influencer-Acquired — All Creators" (Future-Proofing)**

Klaviyo filter criteria:
- `$source` contains "superfiliate" OR "Superfiliate"
- AND Profile created date within last 90 days

Purpose: Catches all influencer-acquired subscribers regardless of specific creator. As Kenetik does more activations, this becomes the reusable influencer audience segment.

**New Segment C: "Cleetus — Converted (30-Day Tracking)"**

Klaviyo filter criteria:
- Member of Segment A (Cleetus All Subscribers)
- AND Has placed order at least once since 2026-03-07

Purpose: Track the 30-day cohort conversion rate — the key metric for evaluating whether the Cleetus activation generated lasting customers vs one-time discount buyers.

**Segment relationship to existing:**

## 13. Learning Notes

**Measurement completed: 2026-04-08 (Day 30) — Outcome: Success**

For the Cleetus activation cohort on Klaviyo segmentation, three segments were built within 1 day of approval, covering 6,345+ identifiable profiles across non-converters (1,102 in S9jbqn) and free-pack-no-repeat purchasers (5,243 in V3eTsC). The existing UE | Cleetus Audience (Yqj4WM) grew to 12,066 profiles.

**Key insight:** Subscriber-level segmentation captures 2.4x more profiles than purchaser-only segmentation for influencer cohorts. Of the March 7 cohort, ~17% (1,102) subscribed but never purchased — invisible to the pre-existing purchaser-only segment. This confirms that influencer activations require full-funnel segmentation from Day 0.

**Data quality gap:** The "Influencer-Acquired — All Creators" segment (U22Pai) has 0 profiles because Klaviyo's `$source` property is not being tagged with "superfiliate"/"Superfiliate" by the Superfiliate integration. This needs investigation before the next creator activation to ensure the reusable all-creators segment works.

**Downstream impact:** CS-2026-001 (Welcome Series routing) was unblocked on Day 1 and is currently measuring through Apr 10. The financial impact of this segmentation work flows through that ChangeSet.
