## Objective

Retire the Watermelon Lime / 6 Pack SKU (R1206-004) from the active subscription base by moving all 86 active WML 6-pack subscriptions onto the 12-Pack (R1212-004) without changing the volume of Kenetik any subscriber receives per period or the price they pay per period.

## Hypothesis

Migrating the 86 active WML 6-pack subscriptions to the 12-pack at volume- and price-neutral terms — doubling order/charge frequency for single-6-pack subs, and collapsing 2x6-pack subs to 1x12-pack with frequency unchanged — lets us retire the WML 6-pack SKU with zero change to per-period cans or per-period spend for each subscriber. Pairing the migration with a same-day proactive customer email minimizes churn from the unexpected catalog/line-item change.

## Scope

86 active subscriptions containing the Watermelon Lime 6-Pack (per export `subscriptions_active-2026.06.08`). Source-of-truth working file: `subscriptions_watermelon-lime_12pack_update_2026-06-08.csv` (86 rows, verified 0 discrepancies on full field-by-field re-check).

**Frequency logic (the volume-neutral mechanism):**

- **82 single-6-pack subscriptions (quantity = 1):** `order_interval_frequency` and `charge_interval_frequency` **doubled**, interval unit unchanged. Examples: 30d->60d, 14d->28d, 2m->4m, 25w->50w, 60d->120d, 1m->2m. Result: 12 cans every 2x the interval = same cans/day at same $/day.
- **4 two-6-pack subscriptions (quantity = 2):** subscription IDs **672347155, 781152509, 788724219, 818666832**. Quantity set **2 -> 1**, frequency **left unchanged** (2x6 = 12 cans already per charge). Result: 1x12-pack at the same cadence.

Net effect: every affected subscriber receives the same number of cans per period at the same price per period; only the SKU/line-item and (for single-pack subs) the cadence number change.
