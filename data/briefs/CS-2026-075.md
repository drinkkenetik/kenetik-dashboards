## Hypothesis

April subscription attach rate is running at 40.7% vs. March's 11.5% — a 254% improvement that appeared at month boundary without a known offer change. Candidate drivers (in order of prior probability): (a) Recharge offer page or upsell module change, (b) checkout UX change, (c) cohort quality shift (lower volume of one-off purchasers → higher attach ratio among remaining buyers), (d) upstream acquisition channel shift favoring subscription-propensity audiences, (e) measurement/attribution bug.

Identifying the lever is potentially the single largest LTV move available this quarter — worth a dedicated RCA before we either lose the effect or fail to scale it.

## Actions

1. Pull Recharge attach-rate data by week across March and April 2026, split by: product, acquisition channel, new vs. returning, AOV band.
2. Diff Shopify theme deploys, checkout script changes, and Recharge offer-page versions between 2026-03-30 and 2026-04-01.
3. Diff Klaviyo flows triggering during the month boundary window.
4. If single variable isolates to the shift, attempt to reverse-engineer the lever and brief Peter/Devon on protection/scaling plan.
5. If no single variable isolates, design an A/B holdback test to validate the hypothesis before May.
