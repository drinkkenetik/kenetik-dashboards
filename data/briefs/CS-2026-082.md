## Context

D18 Trending Analysis surfaced the Matt Hughes FB reel (currently Kenetik's highest-performing paid creative, 9.57x ROAS per CS-2026-045) as attracting hostile comments, including at least one reference to the domestic-violence history from Hughes's personal life. The reel's commercial performance is the reason we scale spend; the comment stream is the reason we cannot scale without a moderation protocol. No current comment-moderation SOP exists for paid reels carrying athlete partnership content.

This collides with CS-2026-045 (Matt Hughes scale decision closing today) and CS-2026-061 (Matt Hughes FB→Paid graduation, asset spec delivery due today). Without a moderation protocol, scaling spend scales the brand-safety exposure linearly.

## Hypothesis

A documented comment-moderation protocol — hide/delete criteria, escalation thresholds, response templates, Meta Brand Safety settings — reduces brand-risk exposure to a level that supports scaling the Matt Hughes creative portfolio without materially slowing approval velocity on future athlete content.

## Scope

1. Audit current comment volume, sentiment distribution, and specific hostile-comment categories on the Matt Hughes reel (Charlotte, 24h)
2. Define hide/delete decision tree: (a) auto-hide keywords list, (b) manual-review escalation criteria, (c) DV-reference policy (Peter, 24h)
3. Response template library: on-brand, non-defensive, redirect-to-product (Charlotte, 48h)
4. Meta Ads Manager configuration: enable Brand Safety keyword block list, enable comment-disabled option where flagged (Katie support, 24h after Peter sign-off)
5. Daily monitoring cadence: Charlotte owns pre-11am review of all athlete-creative comment streams (standing process post-launch)
6. Escalation path to Devon for any DV-reference comment that cannot be hidden within 2 hours

## Measurement Plan

- Primary: % of hostile comments hidden/deleted within 4 hours of posting (target ≥90%)
- Secondary: count of DV-reference comments escalated to Devon (log; no target)
- Secondary: Matt Hughes creative ROAS maintenance post-protocol (control: CS-2026-045 baseline)
- Window: 14 days from protocol activation
- Sample: all comments on all active Matt Hughes paid creatives in the measurement window
