## Problem Statement

38-50 subscribers cancel per month citing "Did not mean to start a subscription" (10.7-14.5% of
all cancels) [src: data/voc/cancellations-corpus.json; diagnostic/retention-teardown.md].

## Audit Finding (read before building)

Kenetik's own autonomous CRO loop already tested the most likely structural cause of this --
a forced or pre-selected subscription default on the PDP buy-box -- and killed it:

- **Cycle 1** (`CS-2026-115`, closed 2026-05-12): made subscription the pre-selected default.
  ATC +5.7%, but checkout CVR -19.2% (guardrail breach). Team conclusion at the time:
  "subscription-first ordering lifts buy-button engagement but adds checkout friction."
  [src: data/performance-memory/pdp-atc.md]
- **Cycle 2** (`CS-2026-154`, reverted 2026-06-15): reordered subscription above one-time
  WITHOUT pre-selecting it. CVR fell further (-25.7%), tier-1 guardrail breach, manually
  reverted same day (PR #84, commit d8482f1). [src: data/performance-memory/pdp-atc.md]

**As of this writing, the live PDP buy-box defaults to one-time purchase, unselected.** The
default-selection lever has already been tested twice and removed. This CS does not propose
touching the buy-box default again.

## Resolution Path (Diagnose / Remove / Refine / Add)

- **Diagnose:** With the default-selection lever already ruled out by CS-2026-115/154, the
  likely remaining cause is label ambiguity at the moment of active selection: "Subscribe & Save
  X%" framing commonly reads as a discount code, not a recurring commitment, unless the
  recurring-billing language is explicit and adjacent to the price.
  **[ASSUMED -- verify against the live current copy; this session did not have direct access to
  the live Shopify theme code.]**
- **Remove:** Nothing further to remove on the default-selection axis -- already done (as a
  revert) in Cycle 2.
- **Refine (this CS):** Add one line of explicit recurring-billing + cancellation-policy copy
  adjacent to the Subscribe & Save selector, wherever it appears (PDP buy-box, cart drawer,
  checkout upsell). No change to which option is pre-selected, no page-structure change --
  materially lower CVR risk than Cycles 1-2 because the CVR-sensitive lever (the default) is
  untouched.
- **Add:** A separate post-purchase confirmation email (`CS-2026-229`) as the safety net for
  whatever ambiguity persists after the copy fix -- justified because it operates entirely
  post-checkout and carries zero ATC/CVR risk, unlike this CS's surface.
