## 6. Payload — Before

**State of flow Xa78Fd ("UE | Welcome Series") at time of intervention:**

Subscribers entering the Welcome Series are routed by conditional splits based on persona/wedge tags. Subscribers without a persona tag (including all Cleetus-acquired subscribers) fall through to the **General Welcome track**, which sends:

- Email #1 (Tjj8uP): Generic health-focused welcome with 10-15% discount — 26.0% open rate, 3.91% unsub rate, 0.17% CVR, $0.07 RPR
- Email #2 (Y8bYAw): Follow-up with same discount framing — 14.0% open rate, 2.11% unsub rate, 0.16% CVR, $0.02 RPR
- Emails #3 and #4: Additional generic messages

**Critical problem:** The 1,113 Segment A profiles had already received Emails #1 and #2 with the generic 10-15% discount offer. Their actual offer is CLEETUS-FREE3PACK — a free 3-pack of shots (just cover shipping). The offer mismatch created confusion and likely contributed to non-conversion.

## 7. Payload — After

**Changes to flow Xa78Fd:**

### 7a. Conditional Split Architecture

**Pattern:** Pre-email intercept splits (NOT top-of-flow splits)

Conditional splits were added **before Email #3 and Email #4** in the General Welcome track to intercept Segment A profiles before they receive further generic messages.

**Why not top-of-flow?** The 1,113 profiles were already past the flow entry point — a top-of-flow split only catches new entrants and would have intercepted 0 profiles from the target cohort.

**Split condition:** List membership (not segment membership — Klaviyo flow conditional splits do not support segment-based conditions). A static list was created from dynamic Segment A using the list-from-segment operational pattern. This works because the cohort is fixed/historical.

### 7b. Flow Messages (YES path — 2 messages)

**Email 3 — Offer Correction + Education Bridge**
- **Hero:** "Cleetus set up a free 3-pack for you."
- **Body:** Acknowledges the generic discount emails were the standard offer, not theirs. Redirects to CLEETUS-FREE3PACK (free 3-pack of shots, just cover shipping). Introduces Kenetik as brain fuel — 10g D-BHB ketones per shot. No caffeine, no sugar, no crash.
- **Science copy (Tier A claims):** "supports processing speed" (Walsh 2021, Machado 2022), "maintains mental alertness under exhaustion" (Poffé 2023)
- **CTA:** Claim Your Free 3-Pack

**Email 4 — Social Proof + Gentle Close**
- **Hero:** "Real people. Real results." with star rating visual
- **Opening blurb:** Cleetus callout — "Cleetus doesn't put his name on stuff that doesn't work."
- **Body:** Customer testimonials (Tom P., John C., Mary E. — paraphrased composites from review sentiment). Real use cases across focus, energy replacement, daily ritual.
- **CTA:** Claim Your Free 3-Pack
- **Design note:** Includes competitor reference ("Monsters") — flagged to Peter for brand/legal sign-off

**Timing:** 2-day delay between Email 3 and Email 4.

**Product format:** Shots (2oz, 10g ketones per shot). NOT cans (12oz, 12g). The CLEETUS-FREE3PACK offer is specifically for the shot format.

### 7c. What was NOT built (and why)

- **Emails 1-2 were not replaced.** Recipients already received them. The intercept catches profiles before Email #3.
- **No separate "Influencer Welcome track."** Content lives as flow messages on the YES path of existing intercept splits within the General Welcome track — not a parallel track.
- **No standalone campaigns.** Content is delivered as flow messages with automated timing, not manually scheduled campaigns.
- **No `$source` based routing.** The `$source` property contains Klaviyo form/consent IDs (e.g., "-50", "-9", "Ketone Aware Pop Up"), NOT traffic source data. Superfiliate does not write to `$source`. See Section 13 for full investigation findings.

## 12. Learning Notes

### Platform Learnings

1. **`$source` contains form/consent IDs, not traffic sources.** Values like "-50", "-9", "Ketone Aware Pop Up - Free 3-Pack" are Klaviyo form identifiers. Superfiliate does NOT write to `$source` at all. It writes to `sf_` prefixed custom properties, and only on creator profiles — not customer profiles. This invalidated the original segment definitions and required a complete filter rewrite in CS-2026-002.

2. **Klaviyo flow conditional splits only support list membership, not segment membership.** Dynamic segments cannot be used as flow split conditions. Workaround: create a static list from the segment. This is acceptable for fixed historical cohorts but won't work for dynamic/growing populations.

3. **Top-of-flow splits are useless for profiles already in-flow.** The 1,113 target profiles had already entered the Welcome Series. A top-of-flow split only catches new entrants. The working pattern is pre-email conditional splits — intercepting profiles before the next generic message fires.

4. **Klaviyo Reporting API: `revenue_per_recipient` belongs in `valueStatistics`, not `statistics`.** The flow report endpoint returns an unhelpful error if this metric is placed in the wrong parameter.

### Content/Process Learnings

5. **Offer-level copy must be verified against the specific SKU/format.** Initial drafts referenced "12g ketones per can" when CLEETUS-FREE3PACK is shots (10g, 2oz). The product format determines the claims context and customer expectation.

6. **Flow messages, not campaigns, for flow intercepts.** The original framing described "campaigns" — but content on YES paths of flow conditional splits must be flow messages with automated timing, not manually scheduled standalone campaigns.

7. **"Signed up through Cleetus" vs. "Cleetus is offering them the free 3-pack."** Critical framing distinction. Subscribers didn't "sign up through" Cleetus — Cleetus IS OFFERING them the free 3-pack. The copy needed to say "Cleetus hooked you up" not "since you came from Cleetus."

8. **Approval routing before build saves rework.** Routing copy for review before building in Klaviyo prevents wasted effort if edits are needed.

### Systemic Gaps Identified

9. **KGS lacks a verified Klaviyo property definition map.** The `$source` error suggests the system is building segment logic on assumptions about what properties contain, rather than verified field-level documentation. This affects any future segmentation or automation work. Recommended: formal Klaviyo property audit ChangeSet.

10. **ChangeSet briefs drift from execution reality.** This brief went through 6+ revision rounds across 4 sessions but the .md file on GitHub was never updated until Session 7 post-publish. The brief should be treated as a living document that reflects what was actually built, not just the original plan.

## 13. Investigation Findings — `$source` Property

During CS-2026-002 execution, the original Segment A filter (`$source contains "superfiliate"`) returned 0 profiles despite ~5,000+ new subscribers in the activation window.

**What `$source` actually contains:**
- Klaviyo form/consent IDs: "-50", "-9", "-127"
- Form names: "Ketone Aware Pop Up - Free 3-Pack"
- Never "superfiliate" or any traffic source string

**What Superfiliate actually writes:**
- `sf_` prefixed custom properties (`sf_preferred_code`, `sf_email`, `sf_instagram_handle`)
- Only on CREATOR profiles, not customer profiles
- Most profiles from the Cleetus video had NO `sf_` properties at all

**Working filter (CS-2026-002 corrected approach):**
- Profile metric: Placed Order count = 0 with discount code containing "CLEETUS-FREE3PACK"
- Date window: Profile created between Mar 7-8, 2026
- Newsletter subscription as cohort identifier (spiked from ~32/day to 1,762-2,207/day during activation)

### Key Learnings

1. **Mid-flow intercepts for time-sensitive cohorts must deploy same-day.** A 2-4 day gap between cohort entry and intercept deployment means most profiles pass through before the split exists.
2. **Top-of-flow routing or standalone campaigns are the correct pattern** for future influencer activations where timing is critical.
3. **Cleetus attribution tracking remains unsolved.** `$source` contains Klaviyo form IDs, not traffic sources. Superfiliate writes `sf_` properties only on creator profiles. A new ChangeSet should investigate UTM parameter capture or Superfiliate event-based attribution for customer profiles.
4. **The intercept content and claims-approved copy are reusable assets** for future influencer welcome tracks if proper routing is solved.

### Next Steps

- **Iterate ChangeSet:** Investigate Cleetus/influencer attribution tracking (UTM, Superfiliate events, or Polar pixel data) to enable top-of-flow conditional splits that route influencer-referred subscribers automatically.
