## Problem

The 2026-03 acquisition cohort (n=5,987) is the second-largest cohort in our 16-month dataset, and its M1 repeat rate is 6.3% — vs 17–20% baseline. The 2026-02 cohort (n=2,497) was already weakest at 12.8% M1; March was lower still. These two consecutive sub-baseline cohorts mean acquisition quality has degraded materially. The 2026-03 cohort is in its M1 repeat window NOW (May 2026); without intervention, the gap will lock in as permanent loss when the window closes mid-month.

At the 6.3% rate, ~376 customers from the Mar cohort will repeat in May vs the ~1,000+ that baseline would predict. The gap is ~624 customers × ~$65 AOV ≈ $22,100–$40,000 in incremental May revenue.

## Hypothesis

Two non-mutually-exclusive root causes:

1. **Acquisition channel mix shift** — Q1 2026 may have over-weighted lower-intent paid traffic (correlated with Pixel ROAS recovery masking conv-volume drop, per L-065). If true, this is a CAC-efficiency signal.
2. **Lifecycle flow miscalibration** — Welcome Series (15% off), 15-day educational, and retention flows are timed/content-calibrated for higher-engagement cohorts. The Mar cohort's lower initial engagement may be falling out of standard sequence touchpoints.

A targeted re-engagement sequence + acquisition-mix audit will distinguish between the two and recover what's recoverable inside the M1 window.

## Scope

**In scope:**

- Build a Klaviyo segment of 2026-03 acquisition cohort (Mar new customers who have not placed a second order as of 5/1).
- Ship a 3-touch re-engagement / repurchase prompt sequence with a clear product-fit reframe (not a generic "we miss you"). Rotate offer tier per touch (no offer → curated bundle suggestion → modest discount on third touch only if needed).
- Audit which existing flows (Welcome, 15-day educational, retention winback) the Feb / Mar cohorts entered and at what completion rate. Identify the highest-drop-off step.
- Pre-stage the Apr 5 spike cohort retention pull for 5/5 (M1 window for that 108-customer subgroup opens 5/5–5/7 per D06 A3).
- Cross-reference Feb 2026 acquisition channel mix (paid vs organic vs referral) against the 12.8% M1 to confirm or rule out channel-mix root cause.

**Out of scope:**

- Acquisition-side fixes (ad creative, audience targeting changes) — separate workstream pending channel-mix audit results.
- Subscription-specific churn save flows — covered by CS-100.
