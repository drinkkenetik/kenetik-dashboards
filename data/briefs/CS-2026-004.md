**Change 2: Superfiliate Attribution Investigation (Nick)**
- Investigate why only 229 of 1,682 Superfiliate-tracked conversions attributed to Superfiliate in Polar
- **Three deterministic attribution signals identified during review:**
  1. **Shopify tag "Superfiliate Conversion Approved"** — order was tracked by Superfiliate AND commission was paid. Present on all approved Superfiliate orders regardless of referral link vs promo code path.
  2. **Shopify tag "Superfiliate Conversion Rejected"** — order was tracked by Superfiliate but commission was NOT paid. Still a Superfiliate-attributed order for channel grouping purposes.
  3. **Promo code "CLEETUS-FREE3PACK"** — definitive Cleetus attribution signal. This code is only applied via Cleetus's YouTube link, auto-applied at cart. The free 3-pack SKU is not listed on the website and cannot be found organically.
- **Hypothesis 3 eliminated:** Users cannot find the free 3-pack SKU through natural search — it's embedded in the Cleetus link only. All free 3-pack orders are definitively Cleetus-attributed.
- **Target upgraded to >90%** (from original >50%) based on three deterministic signals covering all Superfiliate order types.
- Lauren at Polar is actively building a Superfiliate channel grouping using these signals. Custom channel grouping ID: custom_5144.

## 12. Learning Notes

**Closed:** 2026-04-10 | **Outcome:** Success | **Approved by:** Katie

**Learning Note:** For paid media attribution on Polar Analytics, the March 7 exclusion and Superfiliate investigation resulted in two confirmed outcomes:

1. **March 7 Exclusion:** Paid Search ROAS stabilized at 6.66x (up from 5.26x baseline) after excluding the Cleetus activation outlier. No budget misallocation occurred — spend actually decreased from $12,669 to $10,379 over the 30-day window. The exclusion prevented inflated baselines from distorting optimization decisions.

2. **Superfiliate Attribution:** The Polar custom channel grouping (`custom_5144`) is live and operational, using all three deterministic signals identified in this investigation: Shopify tags ("Superfiliate conversion approved" / "Superfiliate conversion rejected") and promo codes (CLEETUS, DOC, ROMAN). Attribution capture went from 14% (click-based) to ~100% (tag-based) for Superfiliate-tagged orders. During the measurement window, Superfiliate showed 300 orders / $16,046 net sales as a distinct channel.

**Key insight:** Tag-based deterministic attribution is the correct model for influencer/creator channels. Click-based attribution severely undercounts (87% gap on March 7) because creator-driven customers often navigate directly to the site or search organically after seeing content, breaking the click chain. Shopify order tags and promo codes provide order-level attribution that is immune to this problem. This finding should inform attribution architecture for all future creator partnerships.
