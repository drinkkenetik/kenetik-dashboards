## Appendix: Study Reference

**Robberechts, R., Bekhuis, Y., et al. (2026).** Post-exercise ketone supplementation improves endurance performance and mitochondrial adaptations during an 8-week endurance training intervention. *The Journal of Physiology*, 0.0, 1–20. DOI: 10.1113/JP290315

**Key findings for flow content:**
- 28 trained male cyclists, 8-week periodized training, randomized placebo-controlled
- Post-exercise + pre-sleep ketone monoester (25g) vs. isocaloric placebo
- 30-min time trial: training +18%, training + KE +22% (4% between-group difference, p<0.001)
- Relative VO2peak: KE group continued improving through week 8 (+12%) while control plateaued at week 3 (+6%)
- Citrate synthase activity (mitochondrial marker): training +15%, training + KE +32% (p=0.035)
- OXPHOS complex II protein: +25% in KE group, unchanged in control (p=0.030)
- Peak cardiac output: similar between groups — performance gains driven by muscular, not cardiac, adaptations
- Blood D-BHB elevated to ~2.6 mM within 30 min post-exercise after KE ingestion

**Claims compliance note:** Study used ketone monoester (KetoneAid), not Kenetik's D-BHB formulation. Same active metabolite but different delivery. Tier A language applies. Devon approval required for all science references in flow content.
