## Access model
Default-deny: everything gated except home, account/auth, and the application forms.

| Customer tag | Access |
|---|---|
| _(none / logged out)_ | Home + apply + account only |
| `Dealer` | + cans, shots, concentrate PDPs |
| `Practitioner` / `Pro` | + everything, incl. the `pro-gated` KenetikPro PDP |

Tags applied by the team in Admin (form review or referral) — server-side, no app proxy.
