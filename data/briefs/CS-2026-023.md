## Hypothesis
Email campaign CTR has collapsed 57% (0.57% vs 1.32% baseline) while flows maintain 4x higher conversion (0.45% vs 0.11%). We hypothesize that campaign content/offer alignment has drifted from subscriber intent, and that adopting hooks and messaging patterns from high-performing SMS and Welcome Series flows will recover CTR to ≥1.0% within 14 days.

- Domain 02: UE Relo Subscription Converter at 6.87% conv / $5.58 RPR

## Scope
1. **Audit Phase (Days 1-3):** Review last 10 campaign subject lines, preview text, and body copy. Compare against Welcome Series SMS hooks and top-performing flow copy.
2. **Test Phase (Days 4-14):** Run 3 A/B tests on next scheduled campaigns:
   - Test A: Subject line style (current vs SMS-inspired hook)
   - Test B: CTA placement and copy (current vs flow-style behavioral CTA)
   - Test C: Segment refresh (full list vs 90-day engaged recency segment)
3. **Consolidation:** Identify and deactivate 0%-engagement flows.

## Measurement Plan
- **Primary Metric:** Campaign CTR
- **Baseline:** 0.57% (Mar 9-16 median)
- **Target:** ≥1.0% CTR (76% improvement, still below 1.32% prior baseline)
- **Secondary Metrics:** Open rate (≥33%), conversion rate (≥0.20%)
- **Window:** 14 days from first test send
- **Success Gate:** CTR ≥1.0% on 2 of 3 test variants

### Additional Learning: Audience Expansion Test (C7)

Kiara tested an expanded audience (Wedge B + first-time purchasers + engaged prospects) on C7. It underperformed. Learning: don't send to first-time purchasers or Wedge B until engagement picks up, or create sub-segments of those groups filtered by actual email engagement.

### Popup A/B Test (Bonus)

Underground Ecom also ran a popup A/B test: 15% off vs 10% off. **15% off was the clear winner on mobile** — higher AOV, more revenue, 38 submits vs 28 on 10%.

### Tests B and C: Not Executed

Test B (CTA placement) and Test C (segment refresh / "clicked in 90 days" segment) were not executed during the measurement window. These remain valid test ideas for future campaigns.

## Outcome Summary

**Partial success.** The SMS-inspired subject line hooks produced meaningful open rate improvement (33.8–45.2% vs 29.3% baseline, well above the 33% target) but only marginal CTR improvement (0.62–0.66% vs 0.57% baseline, well below the 1.0% target).

**Key insight:** Subject lines are getting people to open, but the body content and CTAs are where the conversion gap lives. This points to Test B (CTA placement/copy) as the higher-leverage next experiment. The problem is not awareness (opens) — it's engagement with the email content itself (clicks).

**Revenue generated:** C11 was the standout with 16 orders and $1,499.51 revenue. Combined test revenue: $1,676.62 across 18 orders.

## Learnings
1. SMS-inspired hooks improve open rates significantly but are not sufficient to fix CTR alone
2. The bottleneck is in-email engagement (CTA, body content), not subject line appeal
3. Audience expansion to Wedge B and first-time purchasers does not work without engagement filtering
4. 15% off popup converts better than 10% on mobile
5. Test B (CTA placement) and Test C (segment refresh) should be prioritized in next email optimization cycle
