- **Frontiers in Nutrition 2026 meta-analysis** — Pooled meta-analysis of RCTs found a significant positive effect of exogenous ketones on cognitive performance (SMD = 0.26, 95% CI: 0.11–0.40, p = 0.0007) across healthy adults and neuropsychiatric populations. [src: brain/kenetik-brain/references/claims-compliance.md#focus-cognition-tier-a]
