## Hypothesis

The Matt Hughes "Toughest Battles" TT reel (Apr 29) cleared the universal 3x-reach graduation gate at 7.59x TT-account 30d average. The post earned reach without proportional engagement — likely an algorithm-driven distribution event rather than peer-driven virality (1.32% ER vs. TT median 2.5%). If we apply Matt Hughes paid amplification (which has held 4.6% engagement at 1.66M imp on Meta per CP-001–CP-004), we expect paid TT engagement to land at ≥2% (closer to TT median) with ROAS ≥1.5x in 14 days. Confirms: (a) reach-only graduation candidates can convert when creator equity is strong; (b) Matt Hughes DNA generalizes to TT paid beyond the Apr 24 prank format already covered in CS-2026-151.

**Risk note:** This is a *reach-qualified* graduation, not an ER-qualified one. Conviction is moderate, not high. ER 1.32% is below TT median 2.5% — possible the breadth came from a niche algorithmic surge that won't translate to paid (paid traffic skews different than organic surge audiences). Mitigation: lower budget ($20/day vs. typical $40), shorter measurement (14d), aggressive ROAS pause gate (1.0x at day 7).
