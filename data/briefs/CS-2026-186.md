## §10a — Hypothesis

UE | Subscription Winback (WYiX2k) has been dead since 2025-09-08: 0 clicks / 0 conversions on 136 recipients despite a 41.2% open rate. Three competing hypotheses (broken CTA / suppression overlap / stale 2025 offer) explain the dead flow. Rebuild the flow (fix CTA, refresh content, lead with a skip/frequency offer) and falsify against May voluntary churn (283 events). Recovery materially compresses the active_sub_count timeline to 2,800. This is the largest single recurring-revenue lever in the Q2 model and ships inside existing Klaviyo with no new creative.

## §10b — Resolution Path (Diagnose / Remove / Refine / Add)

- **Diagnose:** Flow opened (41.2%) but produced 0 clicks → CTA link was broken; offer mechanic and copy were stale (unchanged since 2025); winback led with a hard discount that gave cancelling subscribers no lighter-touch option. (Evidence: synthesis 2026-05-29-s1 + Lifecycle ACT-LM-2.)
- **Remove:** No redundant flow to retire — Subscription Winback (WYiX2k) is the only dedicated retention asset. Removed the stale 2025 offer block and the dead CTA.
- **Refine:** Re-tuned the existing flow rather than building net-new — fixed the broken CTA link/URL, refreshed all stale 2025 content (copy, offer, dates, product references) to current.
- **Add:** Added a skip/frequency-change offer as the **new first email** in the sequence — gives a cancelling subscriber a low-friction "pause / change cadence" path before the discount ask, capturing saves that a discount-only flow misses.
