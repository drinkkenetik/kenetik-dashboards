## Hypothesis

Customers who made a one-time purchase using a Brain Awareness Week discount code (Mar 16–22) and do not have an active subscription will be ~3 weeks post-purchase by early April — the natural repurchase window when product is fully depleted. A targeted SMS/email sequence positioned around "running low? lock in your supply" will convert these proven buyers into subscribers at a higher rate than the original BAW urgency framing, because the messaging aligns with a real consumption need rather than promotional pressure. Three weeks is the sweet spot: long enough for full product trial and depletion across all pack sizes, soon enough that the brand experience is still fresh.

## Why This Timing Is Better Than the Original

The original CS-030 brief relied on BAW urgency ("deal ends tomorrow") during the promotional window. That window passed without execution. However, the current timing (early April) is actually a stronger conversion moment:

1. **Product trial complete** — These buyers have had ~3 weeks to experience Kenetik. They know if they like it.
2. **Supply fully depleted** — At 3 weeks out, even 12-packs at every-other-day usage are gone. The "running low" trigger is real, not manufactured.
3. **No promotional noise** — BAW is over, inbox competition is lower, and the message won't get lost in the promotional flood.
4. **Self-selected quality** — Anyone who hasn't already reordered or subscribed is a warm lead who needs one more nudge, not a hard sell.

## Campaign Design

### Touch 1: SMS (Primary — Day 1)
- **Timing:** Apr 6, 10am ET (Monday — start-of-week mindset, 3 weeks post-BAW)
- **Final copy:** "Three weeks since you gave your brain real fuel. Subscribe and it never runs out, 20% off every order for life: [LINK]"
- **CTA:** Direct link to subscription PDP
- **Guardrail:** SMS unsub rate ≤1.0%

### Touch 2: Email (Follow-up — Day 1, 4hr delay)
- **Timing:** Apr 6, 2pm ET
- **Subject line:** "Your brain noticed the difference."
- **Preview text:** "Metabolic brain fuel on your schedule. 20% off every order, for life."
- **Hero headline:** YOUR BRAIN NOTICED THE DIFFERENCE, SO KEEP THE FUEL COMING
- **Hero CTA:** START MY SUBSCRIPTION
- **Body P1:** "During Brain Awareness Week, you tried something different: metabolic brain fuel instead of caffeine, willpower, or nothing at all. Calm clarity without the crash."
- **Body P2 (warm punch):** "That was three weeks ago. The supply is gone, but the standard your brain is holding you to isn't."
- **Subscription section header:** NEVER RUN OUT OF BRAIN FUEL
- **Subscription section body:** "Subscribe and Kenetik shows up on your schedule. Your brain stays fueled, we'll handle the rest."
- **Subscription benefits (checkmarks):** 20% off every order for life | Modify or cancel anytime | Free 6-pack of Kenetik Shots with your second order
- **Bottom CTA:** START MY SUBSCRIPTION
- **Closer (proof string):** 12 grams of ketones. Caffeine-free. Sugar-free. Nothing artificial.
- **Design:** Single above-fold CTA plus matching CTA below subscription section. No navigation, no distractions. Per CS-2026-023 CTA placement learnings.

### Touch 3: SMS Reminder (Day 3 — non-converters only)
- **Timing:** Apr 8, 10am ET (Wednesday)
- **Final copy:** "Your brain already knows. Kenetik on your schedule, 20% off for life, canceled anytime: [LINK]"
- **Only send to:** Profiles who received Touch 1 but have NOT converted
- **Flow logic:** Conditional split at T+48hr checks placed_order since flow entry OR subscription_status = active. Converters exit; non-converters receive Touch 3.

### Dimensional Actuals

- **L1 — Margin:** $133.14 first-order actual ($648 projected LTV margin from 3 subs at 50% DTC) → **below** projected $5,184 LTV margin (−87.5% LTV-adjusted, −97.4% first-order). Flow is net-positive at near-zero marginal cost, just below projected scale.

- **L4 — Learning:** **Partially supported.** Top-of-funnel strong (SMS CTR 27–39%, email 43.3% open) validates the 3-week repurchase-window timing + "running low" framing. Bottom-of-funnel weak (0.61% CVR vs ≥1.5% target) indicates the subscription offer itself (20% off + free 6-pack Shots on 2nd order) did not close for warm BAW buyers. Segment was +65% larger than estimated — **the miss is a conversion problem, not an audience problem.**

### What Changed → What Happened → What We Learned

Launched 3-touch Klaviyo flow (SMS → Email → SMS-reminder) targeting BAW one-time buyers 3 weeks post-purchase with "running low — subscribe" framing; flow reached 495 recipients. → 3 subscriptions ($266.28 first-order / $1,296 projected 6-mo LTV) vs 8–20 target / $5,184 expected LTV (−62.5% floor / −75% LTV). Top-of-funnel strong but flow CVR 0.61% vs 1.5% target; segment +65% larger than estimated. → Timing + framing validated (engagement strong) but the subscription *offer* did not convert warm buyers. The miss is a conversion problem, not an audience problem. Next iteration: test stronger first-order sub incentive (30–50% first month) and/or earlier-in-depletion timing. Flow is net-positive at near-zero cost.
