## Problem
The Mar 2026 cohort (n=**5,987 customers**, the single largest recent cohort) is entering first-renewal window within **2-4 weeks**. Multiple independent signals converge:

1. **D01 Feb 2026 M1 retention = 12.8%** — lowest in 6-month window (n=2,497). If Mar follows Feb pattern, Q2 LTV economics deteriorate materially.
2. **D06 Mar 2026 cohort 30-day LTV = $25.93** — significantly below Feb's $45.33 and Jan's ~$55. Early LTV signal is already weak before first-renewal even fires.
3. **D05 lifecycle attribution gap:** 4 Klaviyo flows returning $0 including Customer Winback (config gap, not true failure). Upcoming Subscription Order Flow is the mechanism that would fire first-renewal save messaging — its segmentation + message variants were not designed for a cohort this size or this LTV profile.
4. **D06 churn +45.2%** this week (61 vs 42) — churn pressure is already elevated; first-renewal spike incoming.
5. **L-045 (S37 governance rule)** — voluntary vs involuntary churn split is unresolved; cannot yet segment "declined payment" from "intentional cancel" which is required for a clean save flow.

Without intervention, the Mar cohort's first-renewal window is likely to fire with default messaging designed for smaller historical cohorts, against a worse-than-baseline LTV, with no voluntary/involuntary discrimination on cancels.

## Approach

### Phase 1: Data prerequisite (48h, by 2026-04-24)
1. **L-045 voluntary/involuntary split (Katie + Garrett, 48h):** Pull cancel reason codes from Recharge admin export. Map to two cohorts: (a) voluntary cancels (intent to stop), (b) involuntary (payment failure, card expiry, shipping address invalid). Feed split into Klaviyo segments.
2. **Mar 2026 cohort segment (Katie, 24h):** Build Klaviyo segment `mar-2026-first-renewal` from Recharge subscription list — anyone whose subscription was initiated in Mar 2026 and whose next charge date falls in the next 30 days.
3. **LTV attribution baseline (Garrett):** Confirm Polar attribution captures Upcoming Subscription Order Flow so we can measure this CS's lift.

### Phase 2: Flow extension (48-72h, by 2026-04-25)
4. **Add Mar-cohort branch** to Upcoming Subscription Order Flow:
   - Trigger: subscription renewal scheduled within 7 days AND subscriber in `mar-2026-first-renewal` segment.
   - Message variants (3): (a) flavor variety inviter — "your next shipment: here's what else to try" (uses GP-LAUNCH-2026 Golden Pear + any other flavor introduced since Mar), (b) social-proof reminder — "what athletes are saying" with Joelle Debban + Rockrr Racing UGC, (c) science-forward — "here's what BHB actually did for you this month" (PMID 41994946 Virta 2-yr framing, Brandon-claims-checked).
   - Send cadence: T-7 days, T-3 days, T-1 day before next charge.
   - SMS variants: 2 (reminder + variety inviter at T-2 and T-1 if SMS-subscribed).
5. **Claims review (Brandon, same-day on each draft):** Science-forward variant requires claims-check; Tier 1 evidence cited, no overreach.

### Phase 3: Involuntary churn save (parallel)
6. **Branch for involuntary cancels:** Retry window + payment update nudge sequence fires automatically BEFORE cancel status is logged. Recharge already supports dunning; this CS instruments the Klaviyo-side nudge alongside. Separate messaging from voluntary cancels (tone: "your payment didn't go through — here's how to fix it" vs "here's why your next shipment is worth it").

### Phase 4: Measurement (7-30d)
7. **Primary metric:** Mar 2026 cohort M1 retention rate vs Feb 2026 baseline (12.8%). Target ≥18% (+5.2pp lift).
8. **Secondary:** First-renewal flow CTR, discount-code redemption, involuntary-churn recovery rate.
9. **Pause-instead-of-cancel conversion:** Target ≥25% of voluntary cancel attempts convert to pause.
