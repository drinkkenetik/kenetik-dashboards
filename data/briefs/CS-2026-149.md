## §10a — Hypothesis

Week 5/4 (5/4-5/10) subscription churn spiked to 108 cancellations vs a trailing 3-week baseline of ~67/wk (avg of weeks 4/13, 4/20, 4/27 = 73 + 66 + 63 / 3 = 67.3). That is 1.60x the baseline — exceeds the 150% threshold that triggers DS-07. Activations rose +20.5% WoW in the same window (100 vs 83), so net change was only -8, but gross churn is the operative signal.

**Primary hypothesis:** A meaningful share (>50%) of week 5/4 cancellations is **voluntary / engagement-driven**, not involuntary / payment-failure. Three indicators support this:

1. Activations and charges both rose WoW (+20.5% activations, +9.8% subscription_charge_count). If the cohort was failing on payment, charge volume would be flat or declining, not rising.
2. Recharge Pro recovery baseline 57-64% historically would absorb the majority of involuntary (payment-failure) churn, so an involuntary spike of this magnitude would show up post-recovery — but we see it pre-recovery.
3. The MDay BIG BEAT 5/10 explicitly excluded UE Recharge Subscribers Active — the cohort that churned week 5/4 received NO MDay reactivation touch.

**Decision riding on this RCA:** If voluntary > 50%, the MDay 5/10 subs-exclusion design was a missed reactivation opportunity and Memorial Day 5/18-5/26 BIG QUARTERLY BEAT MUST include the subscriber audience. If voluntary ≤ 50%, the MDay design was correct and Memorial Day should preserve the exclusion (dunning recovery flow does the work).
