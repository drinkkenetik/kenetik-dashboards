### Scope selected

### Q1: What specific solution(s) do you plan to build using the Amazon Ads API?

> Kenetik is a direct-to-consumer beverage brand running our own Amazon advertising. We're building an internal advertising performance pipeline that ingests Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands report data into our company's data warehouse for analysis by our internal marketing and operations team.
>
> Specifically, the pipeline pulls daily search-term reports, campaign performance reports, and keyword performance reports via the Reporting endpoints. Aggregated metrics (impressions, clicks, spend, ACOS, ROAS, CTR, CPC) are joined with our other paid-media channels (Meta, Google, TikTok) in a unified internal dashboard that our CGO and director of digital marketing use for daily decision-making.
>
> This solution is for Kenetik's own advertising account only. It is not resold, redistributed, or made available to third parties.

### Q2: Which advertising processes are you aiming to automate?

> Five internal processes, all aimed at faster decision-making by our marketing team:
>
> 1. Daily search-term performance reporting — automated ingestion of search-term reports (Sponsored Products + Sponsored Brands) so our team has same-day visibility instead of manual UI exports.
>
> 2. Negative keyword identification — surfacing high-spend search terms with no recent conversions for our keyword-management workflow.
>
> 3. Cross-channel ROAS comparison — joining Amazon Ads metrics with Meta, Google, and TikTok in a single internal dashboard so budget reallocation decisions are based on apples-to-apples efficiency.
>
> 4. Performance anomaly detection — week-over-week monitoring of spend and ACOS at the campaign level so unusual drops or spikes trigger an internal review.
>
> 5. Daily executive reporting — including Amazon Ads efficiency in our CGO's daily ops snapshot alongside other DTC channels.
>
> All automation is for internal team use within Kenetik. We are not building a customer-facing tool or third-party software product.
