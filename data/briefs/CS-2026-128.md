## §10b RESOLUTION PATH ANALYSIS

**Diagnose:** Two parallel content briefs are running for overlapping audiences:
1. DS-04 MHAM ("More Good Days Together") May Campaign — Charlotte owns
2. DS-04 GLP-1 cognitive bridge — Lawrence owns

D18 trend signals + D17 GLP-1 trajectory + D10 customer language ("stay sharp," "brain fueled," "CLEAN energy") all converge on **one consumer**: the metabolically-aware adult seeking cognitive resilience. The Frontiers 2026 meta-analysis on exogenous ketones + cognition is the same evidence layer. Running these as two separate production briefs duplicates capture, claims review, and asset creation.

**Remove:** Discontinue treating MHAM and GLP-1 cognitive bridge as separate briefs. Eliminate duplicate production, duplicate claims review, duplicate asset creation. (Resolution Hierarchy Step 2.)

**Refine:** Take the existing in-flight content components from both briefs and refine into a single MHAM-anchored series threading stress/cortisol → cognitive resilience → ketone substrate. Reuse asset specs already commissioned. (Step 3.)

**Add:** Only the consolidated series spec — which itself reduces total work by collapsing two parallel briefs into one. (Step 4 — justified because removal/refinement alone don't generate the consolidated brief; the consolidated form must be authored.)

**Why this CS earns its place:** Net production load is reduced, not added. The CS is the formal record of the consolidation decision and gives a single owner accountability for the joint launch.
