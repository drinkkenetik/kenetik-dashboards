## §10b RESOLUTION PATH ANALYSIS

**Diagnose:** Two parallel content briefs are running for overlapping audiences:
1. DS-04 MHAM ("More Good Days Together") May Campaign — Charlotte owns

### The risk surface

GLP-1 is a drug class (semaglutide, tirzepatide). Any framing that implies *interaction*, *therapy augmentation*, *substitution*, or *condition-management benefit* in the GLP-1 patient population crosses from positioning into a drug/therapy claim. The matrix permits zero of those.

| Framing | Risk | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| *"For adults managing weight, energy, metabolic baseline"* (no GLP-1 mention) | Generic — same as our existing Wedge B framing | ✅ Approved |
| *"Ketones as a metabolic complement"* (positioning, no condition reference) | Tier 2 category framing, consistent with "Metabolic Brain Fuel" tagline | ✅ Approved |
| *"With or alongside GLP-1"* (parenthetical) | Implies safe co-use / co-administration; no clinical evidence backs this for our products specifically | ⚠️ **Reject as written.** Reframe needed. |
| *"For people on GLP-1 / during GLP-1 therapy"* (conditional inclusion) | Directly identifies a drug class + therapy population; crosses into intended-use territory | ⛔ **Hard reject.** Not approvable without medically-supervised KenetikPro framing + clinical-context disclaimers. |
| #GLP1 hashtag participation | Tag itself is descriptive; participating in a public conversation tag isn't a claim, but it routes us into a feed where "Kenetik for GLP-1 users" reads as the implied promise. | ⚠️ Conditional. See below. |
| *"Cognitive complement to metabolic-health protocols"* | Generic, doesn't mention GLP-1 specifically | ✅ Approved |
