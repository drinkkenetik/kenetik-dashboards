## Public Response Draft + Compliance Log + Matt Hughes Gate — 2026-04-28 (Devon via Claude)

**Author:** Claude on Devon's behalf, for Devon + Brandon final sign-off before any post.
**Reviewed against:** `references/claims-compliance.md` v2026-04-20 (Banned Phrases — Hard Block: disease/condition names; "treats / prevents / cures"); `references/brand-voice.md` v1.2 (calm, premium, not salesy).
**Source:** Apr 26 Facebook public comment claiming Kenetik helped with "epilepsy / brain trauma / boxing syndrome."

### Posture

Three principles drove the draft:

1. **No engagement with the medical claims.** We do not confirm, endorse, deny, or speculate on whether Kenetik affected the commenter's epilepsy, TBI, or post-concussion symptoms. Doing any of those, even sympathetically, anchors the brand to a disease-state claim.
2. **Warmth without weight.** The commenter shared a personal story. Coldness reads worse than carefulness. The reply acknowledges them as a person and redirects without making it a brand statement.
3. **Move the conversation off the public thread.** Anything substantive happens by DM with a human, not in a public Facebook reply.

### Public reply — DRAFT (for Devon + Brandon final approval before posting)

> Thank you for taking the time to share this with us — we read every message. Kenetik is built to support everyday focus and clarity, and we don't position it for any medical condition. We can't speak to specific health situations in a public space, but we'd love to learn more about how you're using Kenetik day to day if you'd like to send us a DM.
>
> — Team Kenetik

**Notes on the draft:**
- Opens with acknowledgment, not avoidance.
- Anchors product positioning to the approved category: *"everyday focus and clarity"* — pulls from the Tagline Ladder ("Clarity and Focus on Demand"). No claim, no disease reference.
- *"We don't position it for any medical condition"* is the explicit guardrail line. It does the work of saying "we are not in that lane" without saying "Kenetik does not help with X" (which would imply we have an opinion on the medical effect).
- *"Can't speak to specific health situations in a public space"* is the redirect. It's the same posture we'd use for any disease-adjacent comment — not specific to this poster.
- DM offer is the off-ramp. If they engage by DM, **route to Brandon for the response** — not Katie, not customer service. Brandon is the only person who should reply to a TBI/epilepsy DM.
- Length and warmth match the brand-voice "calm clarity, not salesy" posture — no exclamation points, no marketing CTA, no link.
