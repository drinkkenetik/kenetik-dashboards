## Hypothesis

A single Klaviyo email sent 2-3 days after any first order that included a subscription, before
the first renewal charge, that plainly confirms subscription terms (frequency, next charge date,
amount) and offers a one-click modify/pause/cancel link, will catch a share of accidental
sign-ups before they escalate into confused, post-charge cancellations, refund requests, or
chargebacks -- independent of and complementary to `CS-2026-228`'s checkout-copy fix.

## Evidence

- 38-50 subscribers/month cite "Did not mean to start a subscription"
  [src: data/voc/cancellations-corpus.json; diagnostic/retention-teardown.md]
- The buy-box default is already one-time/unselected (per `data/performance-memory/pdp-atc.md`
  Cycle 1/2 history, see `CS-2026-228` for detail) -- so this touch is not correcting a forced
  opt-in, it is catching residual ambiguity in a customer-initiated choice, or a case where the
  customer changed their mind shortly after ordering and has no easy way to act on it before the
  first renewal.

## Resolution Path (Diagnose / Remove / Refine / Add)

This is the **Add** step of the Resolution Hierarchy as applied to accidental-subscription
prevention (see `CS-2026-228` for the full Diagnose/Remove/Refine analysis on this cancel
reason). Adding a new touchpoint is justified here specifically because:
1. It operates entirely post-checkout, so it carries zero add-to-cart or checkout-conversion
   risk -- unlike the PDP/checkout surface, which the team's own experimentation history
   (CS-2026-115/143/154) shows is CVR-sensitive.
2. No existing Klaviyo flow currently performs this specific confirmation function (verified
   against `klaviyo-automation/triggers.json` at a glance during this session; a full flow-list
   audit was not performed and should precede build).

## Actions

2. Confirm subscription frequency, next charge date, and amount are available as Recharge event

4. QA: confirm the modify/pause/cancel link routes correctly to the Recharge self-service

## Measurement Plan

- **Primary metric:** monthly count of "Did not mean to start a subscription" cancel reason
  (shared metric with CS-2026-228 -- see Dependencies below for how to attribute effect between
  the two)
- **Secondary metric:** refund rate and chargeback rate on first-subscription orders (this touch
  should reduce both even where it doesn't prevent a cancellation outright, since it converts a
  surprise post-charge cancellation into a pre-charge, no-drama one)
- **Window:** 30 days post-launch
- **Success gate:** accidental-cancel-reason count down, refund rate on affected cohort down

## Risk Assessment

- **Risk:** Low. New email send only; no checkout/PDP surface touched.
- **Mitigation:** Cap to one send per customer per subscription start (do not repeat on every
  renewal) to avoid becoming a nagging/low-trust touch.
- **Rollback:** Disable the flow trigger; no other system affected.
