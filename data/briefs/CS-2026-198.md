## Hypothesis
A purpose-built, single-offer evergreen landing page converts paid prospecting traffic better than sending cold ad clicks to the Kenetik home page. The home page is built for brand breadth and returning-visitor navigation; cold prospecting clicks need one linear argument (problem → mechanism → proof → single offer) and one decision (Subscribe & Save vs. One-Time). Hypothesis: routing prospecting traffic to `page.kenetik-evergreen-lp` instead of the home page produces a measurable lift in new-visitor landing-page → purchase conversion at equal or better blended efficiency.

## The change (before → after)
- **Before:** Paid prospecting ads point to the Kenetik home page as the post-click destination.
- **After:** A/B test in the prospecting sandbox splits prospecting traffic between (A) home page [control] and (B) `page.kenetik-evergreen-lp` [variant], holding audiences/creative/budget logic constant.
- **Artifact shipped:** new Shopify page template `templates/page.kenetik-evergreen-lp.json` + 12 sections (`sections/kenetik-lp-*.liquid`), shared assets (`assets/kenetik-lp.css/.js`, `kenetik-3variety.png`), and `snippets/kenetik-lp-icon.liquid`, on branch `feature/evergreen-offer-lp` of `drinkkenetik/kenetik-shopify-theme` (PR into `main` open). Offer = Original Variety 6-pack: Subscribe & Save $23.95 (20% / 30-day plan) vs. One-Time $29.94 [src: products-formats.md#cans-rtd; pricing confirmed by Katie].
