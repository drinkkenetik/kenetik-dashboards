## Hypothesis

The existing "Review request" flow (Klaviyo flow `Rcgah9`, live since May 2024) has a 24.8% open rate — 12+ percentage points below the First Time Post Purchase flow (37.0%) and Repeat Purchaser Post-Purchase flow (38.0%) running on the same audience. If we investigate the flow architecture, timing, segmentation, and messaging — and identify specific optimization levers — we can design follow-up implementation ChangeSets that lift review submission rate to ≥10% and open rate to ≥35%.

## Verdict: HYPOTHESIS SUPPORTED — Multiple optimization levers identified

The review flow is underperforming due to a combination of stale content (untouched since April 2025), a winning variant that was never scaled, missing incentive hooks, and an SMS-to-review UX gap. The review submission rate is **3.7%** and declining. Five concrete optimization paths were identified.

### Finding 3: "Copy of" Variant Is Outperforming 2x — Never Scaled

The most actionable finding. "Copy of Review request email" (TagDHr) consistently outperforms the original across every metric:

| Metric | Original (UM7Vi7) | "Copy of" (TagDHr) | Delta |
|--------|-------------------|---------------------|-------|
| Click-to-open rate | 9.6% | **12.7%** | +32% |
| Conversion rate | 1.6% | **2.0%** | +25% |
| Revenue per recipient | $1.19 | **$1.66** | +39% |

This pattern holds in both 30-day and 90-day windows. The variant is likely a design/copy iteration that someone tested but never promoted to primary. It's only receiving 29% of traffic (401 vs 994 recipients). **Scaling this to 100% of traffic is the single highest-ROI quick win.**

Estimated impact of scaling: If all 1,395 initial recipients (994+401) received the winning variant at its current $1.66 RPR (vs the blended $1.30), incremental monthly revenue = ~$502/month = **~$6,024/year** with zero additional spend.

### Finding 4: SMS Has 33.5% Click Rate But 0.6% Conversion — UX Gap

SMS recipients are highly engaged (33.5% click — excellent for SMS). But only 0.6% convert. This means 110 people clicked the SMS link in the last 30 days, but only 2 completed a review.

**Root cause hypothesis:** The review submission page accessed via SMS link is likely not mobile-optimized, or the link drops the customer at a generic page rather than a pre-populated product review form. This is a UX/landing page issue, not a messaging issue.

### Finding 5: No LoyaltyLion Incentive in the Flow

Kenetik has LoyaltyLion fully integrated with Klaviyo (confirmed: 12 LoyaltyLion metrics active, including "Rule Completed - Make a purchase", "Enrolled", "Reward Available", etc.). However, the review request flow offers **zero incentive** for leaving a review.

Industry benchmark: CPG brands offering loyalty points for reviews see 2-4x higher submission rates. Adding "Earn 50 Kenetik loyalty points for your review" is a standard best practice lever.

**Note:** LoyaltyLion has a "Rule Completed" mechanic that could be wired to the "Submitted review" metric — no manual fulfillment needed.

### Finding 6: Open Rate Has Improved Recently But Still Lags

| Timeframe | Open Rate | Click Rate |
|-----------|-----------|------------|
| 90-day average | 24.8% | 3.7% |
| 30-day average | 29.7% | 5.8% |

The 30-day improvement (29.7% vs 24.8%) may reflect seasonality, Apple MPP normalization, or a recent minor tweak. Still lags the post-purchase benchmark (37-38%).

## Recommendations — Prioritized Implementation ChangeSets

### 1. QUICK WIN: Scale Winning Variant (CS-2026-053)
- **Action:** Route 100% of initial review requests to "Copy of Review request email" (TagDHr). Retire original (UM7Vi7).
- **Expected lift:** +$502/month revenue (+39% RPR), +32% CTO
- **Effort:** 15 minutes in Klaviyo
- **Risk:** Low — variant already proven on 401 recipients/month for 90+ days
- **Subtype:** Quick

### 2. LoyaltyLion Review Incentive Test (CS-2026-054)
- **Action:** Add "Earn 50 Kenetik Loyalty Points" incentive to all review request messages. Wire LoyaltyLion rule to "Submitted review" metric for automatic point award.
- **Expected lift:** 2-4x review submission rate (3.7% → 7-15%)
- **Effort:** 2-4 hours (email copy update + LoyaltyLion rule configuration)
- **Risk:** Low — points cost is negligible (~$0.50/review in reward value)
- **Subtype:** Implementation

### 3. SMS Review Landing Page Audit (CS-2026-055)
- **Action:** Audit the SMS review CTA destination URL. Verify mobile optimization, pre-population of product data, and single-tap review submission capability.
- **Expected lift:** SMS CVR from 0.6% → 5%+ (recovering 5-10 additional reviews/month)
- **Effort:** 1-2 hours investigation + potential URL/redirect fix
- **Risk:** Low
- **Subtype:** Investigation → Implementation
