## 12. Learning Notes (added 2026-05-01 via /changeset-measurement)

**Status:** closed · **Outcome:** underperformed · **Closed:** 2026-05-01

**What changed:** Sent the Earth Day "Clean Fuel for a Cleaner Planet" email to Engaged 3M + Prospects Engaged segments at 2pm ET on 2026-04-22.

**What happened:**

| Metric | Expected | Actual |
|---|---|---|
| Audience (post-suppression) | ~51,643 | 16,108 |
| Open rate | — | 45.87% (7,384 unique) |
| **CTR (primary)** | **≥2.0%** | **0.876%** (141 unique clicks) |
| Click-to-open | — | 1.91% |
| Conversions | — | 13 ($108.86 AOV) |
| **Revenue** | $1,800 expected | **$1,415.16** (-21%) |
| **Margin** (DTC 50%) | $900 | **$707.58** (-21%) |
| Unsub / Bounce / Spam | — | 0.36% / 0.07% / 0 |
| Revenue per recipient | $0.035 (implied) | **$0.088** (+151%) |

**What we learned:**

1. **Cultural hook tiering is finer than "cultural beats science."** Earth Day cleared the science-content baseline (0.88% CTR vs ~0.4% avg, a +119% relative lift) but underperformed our last cultural benchmark, the DST email (1.14% CTR). Cultural saturation matters — a culturally heavy moment like DST that ties directly to bodily state (sleep / energy) outperforms a values-aligned moment (Earth Day) for Kenetik's audience. Recalibrate the CTR target for next values-aligned cultural send to **1.0–1.3%**, not ≥2.0%.

2. **Brief-stage audience estimates need a suppression haircut.** The brief estimated ~51,643 recipients; actual delivered audience was 16,097 — about 31% of the estimate. The miss came almost entirely from suppression segments (Recharge subs, recent purchasers, dead/inactive lists, hard bounces, Apple Privacy unengaged). Future Klaviyo CSes should apply a **~30–35% suppression haircut to segment-size estimates** before computing expected_impact gross revenue.

3. **Per-recipient performance was strong — the loss is a scale issue, not an engagement one.** $0.088 revenue/recipient was 2.5x the brief's implied $/recipient, and 9.2% of clickers converted at a healthy $108.86 AOV. The body content and design didn't fail the audience that did engage; the funnel just narrowed sharply at click.

**Hypothesis verdict:** Partially supported. Cultural hooks beat education for CTR — directionally confirmed. But cultural hooks are not interchangeable; Earth Day < DST for our segment.

**Iterate:** Feed both calibrations (cultural-hook target tier + audience suppression haircut) into the next cultural-seasonal Klaviyo brief. No new variant ChangeSet spawned today.
