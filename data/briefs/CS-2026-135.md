## Problem

The wholesale at-risk pool is $816,553 in historical revenue across 59 accounts. 123 accounts have already churned representing $181,000 in lost revenue — meaning the at-risk pool is 4.5x the churned pool by revenue. With wholesale at $110,486 in April (-6.8% MoM but +43.4% since Jan), the channel is structurally growing while a large pool of high-value accounts is silently slipping. There is no current systematic re-engagement protocol for the at-risk segment.

## Hypothesis

A meaningful share of at-risk accounts can be re-engaged with direct, root-cause-aware outreach. Three dominant root-cause categories likely: order-cadence gap (no obvious dissatisfaction, just slipping cadence), unresolved complaint or quality issue, and competitor switch (competitive shelf-space pressure is now confirmed funded — Neutonic + adjacent products). Differentiated outreach by category should outperform generic check-ins.

## Scope

**In scope:**

- Score 59 at-risk accounts on (a) historical revenue, (b) recency of last order. Rank.
- Direct outreach to top 10 by revenue this week. Each outreach attempts to identify root cause: cadence gap / complaint / competitor switch.
- Use Dierbergs and Genesis launches as a re-engagement narrative ("we have momentum and new placements; here's what's relevant for your set").
- Track recovery rate per category to inform a repeatable triage protocol for the remaining 49 accounts.

**Out of scope:**

- Already-churned accounts ($181K, 123 accounts) — separate workstream, lower expected recovery.
- Distribution / merchandising changes — Josh's separate workstream.

## Success criteria

- Top 10 at-risk accounts contacted within 14 days (by 2026-05-15).
- Root cause identified for ≥7 of 10 contacted accounts.
- Recovery rate measured at 2026-06-15 (45 days from CS launch). Target: 20% of at-risk pool recovered = ~$163K annualized.

## Measurement window

**Window end:** 2026-07-01.
**Interim check:** 2026-05-15 (outreach complete on top 10, root-cause map drafted).
