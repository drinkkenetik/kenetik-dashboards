## Objective

Convert 2,500+ Cleetus McFarland-acquired customers (Mar 7–8 cohort) into retained Kenetik customers through a targeted reorder campaign sequence. These customers were acquired at ~$0 margin through deep-discount codes. Their entire LTV depends on whether they reorder at or near full price.

## Context & Evidence

- Mar 7–8: 4,826 orders, 84% new customers → ~4,000 new-to-file customers
- Initial purchase AOV: $5.84–$9.88 (deep discount codes: CLEETUS, DOC, ROMAN)
- Subscription conversion during activation: ~1.2% (55 new subs from ~4,800 orders)
- Welcome Series unsub rate for this cohort: 3.28% (6x industry benchmark)
- Post-Purchase flow unsub rate: 1.49% (3x benchmark)
- Critical retention window: Day 14–30 post-purchase (Mar 21–Apr 7)
- Email/SMS engaged customers have 6x the AOV of discount-acquired customers ($74.54 vs $12.01)

## Why This Matters (Optimization Hierarchy)

This is an **L3 — Customer Quality** play. If the Cleetus cohort doesn't reorder, the entire activation was a cost center (gross margin near $0 on initial purchases). If even 10% reorder at full price ($45–65 AOV), that's 400+ retained customers generating $18K–$26K in second-purchase revenue with healthy margins.

## Payload

### Before (Current State)
Cleetus-acquired customers enter the standard Welcome Series and Post-Purchase flows. These flows are designed for organic/paid-acquired customers and don't address:
- The brand bridge from Cleetus → Kenetik
- The discount-to-full-price transition
- The subscription upsell opportunity from trial

### After (Proposed State)
A dedicated 3-touch reorder sequence for the Cleetus cohort (requires CS-2026-002 segment to be live):

**Touch 1 — Day 14 (Mar 21):**
- Channel: SMS (primary) + Email (backup)
- Message: "How's your Kenetik working? [social proof from Cleetus + Kenetik science]"
- CTA: Reorder with 15% returning customer discount (not free — bridge pricing)
- Goal: Re-engagement + brand education

**Touch 2 — Day 21 (Mar 28):**
- Channel: SMS
- Message: Product education — what ketones actually do for your brain/body
- CTA: Subscribe & save 20% (shift from one-time to recurring)
- Goal: Subscription conversion

**Touch 3 — Day 30 (Apr 7):**
- Channel: Email (longer format)
- Message: Full brand story — science, mission, community
- CTA: Last chance for returning customer offer
- Goal: Final conversion attempt + brand imprinting

### Dependencies
- **CS-2026-002** (Influencer Subscriber Segmentation) — MUST be live before this campaign can target the right cohort
- Peter: Creative brief for brand-bridge messaging (Cleetus voice → Kenetik voice transition)

## Rollback Plan
If Touch 1 produces >2% unsub rate on the Cleetus cohort, pause Touches 2 and 3. The cohort may be too discount-conditioned to respond to any follow-up, and additional touches would only accelerate list degradation.

## Publish Plan
- **Day 0 (Today):** Route for approval (Katie + Peter)
- **Day 1–2:** Peter drafts creative for 3 touches
- **Day 2–3:** Build flows in Klaviyo (agent execution: ~2 hours once creative is approved)
- **Day 12 (Mar 21):** Touch 1 fires automatically at Day 14 post-purchase
- **Day 19 (Mar 28):** Touch 2 fires
- **Day 28 (Apr 7):** Touch 3 fires

Timeline is driven by the fixed Day 14 reorder window — Touch 1 MUST fire by Mar 21 to catch the cohort in their natural reorder consideration period.

## Measurement Plan
- **Primary metric:** Reorder rate of Cleetus cohort at Day 30 and Day 60
- **Secondary metrics:** RPR per touch, unsub rate per touch, subscription conversion rate
- **Measurement window:** 60 days (full two-cycle measurement)
- **Success threshold:** >8% reorder rate at Day 60 (vs 0% baseline for untouched cohort)
- **Baseline comparison:** Organic-acquired customers from same period for reorder rate benchmarking

## Expected Impact
- **Revenue upside:** $26,000 (if 15% of 4,000 customers reorder at $45 AOV)
- **Revenue expected:** $14,400 (if 8% reorder at $45 AOV)
- **Revenue downside:** $4,500 (if 2.5% reorder at $45 AOV)
- **Margin:** ~50% on full-price reorders = $7,200 expected margin impact
