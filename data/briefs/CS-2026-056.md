## Hypothesis
If we expand the D04 Polar Analytics pull to include campaign-level adds_to_cart and initiate_checkout metrics, then the paid media restructuring decision tree (Fix C) will have the mid-funnel data it needs to make accurate ROAS attribution and campaign-level optimization decisions.

## Background
Performance Memory entry L-003 identified a systematic gap: D04 scans pull top-of-funnel (impressions, clicks, spend, ROAS) and bottom-of-funnel (orders, revenue) from Polar, but not mid-funnel. The paid media restructuring analysis relies on campaign-level ATC rate and checkout initiation rate to distinguish between creative quality problems (low ATC) and checkout friction problems (low checkout rate). Without these signals, the diagnosis defaults to guesswork.

Evidence from L-003: "D04 should pull ATC metrics at campaign level for restructuring analysis — current Polar pulls don't include mid-funnel signals needed by the new restructuring decision tree (Fix C)."

## Proposed Change
Expand the D04 domain scan script to:
1. Pull `adds_to_cart` at campaign level from Polar Analytics
2. Pull `checkout_initiations` at campaign level from Polar Analytics
3. Include ATC rate (adds_to_cart / sessions) and checkout rate (checkout_initiations / adds_to_cart) in D04 output
4. Route these metrics to the D04 brief summary for use in restructuring analysis

## Measurement Plan
- **Baseline:** D04 currently produces 0 mid-funnel signals per scan
- **Target:** 100% of active paid campaigns include ATC rate and checkout rate in next D04 scan
- **Measurement window:** 1 session (verify in next pipeline run)
- **Primary KPI:** Mid-funnel signals present in D04 output = yes/no

## Expected Impact
- No direct revenue impact
- Prevents bad restructuring decisions from missing funnel signals
- Enables data-driven ROAS attribution vs. gut-feel diagnosis
