### Close-Out Correction — 2026-04-20 (same day)

**Outcome revised:** `complete` → `partial`

**Why:** Kenetik opted out of sponsoring Cleetus's car for the Kansas race, and the planned on-camera koozie promotion was tied to that car sponsorship. **Revised learning note:** A-list activations structurally tied to another deal (car sponsorship, tour partnership, etc.) need a backup plan if the parent deal falls through. For bundles with short sell-through windows, decouple the merchandising launch from the sponsorship where possible, or build a standalone activation path (unboxing from home, story content, paid media) that doesn't depend on event-day attendance. Talladega 4/24-25 is now the real first-activation test for the Pit Crew Bundle.
