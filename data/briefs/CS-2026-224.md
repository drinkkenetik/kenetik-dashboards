## Hypothesis

Capturing access-preference + PCP contact on the patient intake form, and routing patient-referred PCPs through a dedicated auto-tagged practitioner application, lets the team correctly triage B1 (connect-with-partner) vs B2 (own-PCP) patients and cleanly attribute patient-originated practitioners — unblocking the KenetikPro physician-referral Path B2 funnel and its contribution to new patient accounts.

## Change payload (before → after)

**Change 1 — Patient "Pro Access Request" form** · pro.drinkkenetik.com · Form ID 1038261 · routes to `pro@drinkkenetik.com`

Added four fields (access preference + PCP info stored as **customer metafields** on the profile, not just the notification email):

1. *"What are you hoping to use KenetikPro for?"* — short text, optional, wellness-framed examples ("general wellness, brain/cognitive performance, a specific goal, or other") for partner-matching.
2. *"How would you like to access KenetikPro?"* — dropdown (Required): **"Be referred to an approved Kenetik practitioner partner"** | **"Have my PCP join the program."**
3. *"PCP Full Name"* — short text, optional.
4. *"PCP Email"* — email, optional. → the patient↔PCP join key.

**Change 2 — new "KenetikPro Practitioner Application: Patient Referral"** · kenetikwholesale.com · Form ID 1061806 · routes to `wholesale@drinkkenetik.com`

Duplicate of the general practitioner application (Form ID 1038318), Company+customer mode, same fields (Email, First/Last name, Company/Practice name, Company Shipping Address, Upload Credentials). **Auto-applies customer tag `referred-by-patient` on submit.** Header set to **"Join KenetikPro"** / *"Your patient would like access to KenetikPro under your care. Apply below to join our practitioner program."* Currently **Inactive — pending activation.**

## Resolution Path (Diagnose / Remove / Refine / Add)

- **Diagnose:** intake forms could not distinguish a B1 patient (no practitioner) from a B2 patient (own PCP), and there was no clean, attributable way to bring a patient-referred PCP into Path C credential review.
- **Remove:** n/a — nothing removed.
- **Refine:** reframed the patient access choice to partner-referral vs. PCP-joins-the-program; store access preference + PCP contact as durable customer metafields.
- **Add:** dedicated patient-referred practitioner application with submit-time `referred-by-patient` auto-tag; patient↔PCP linkage by PCP email (no redundant referring-patient field).
