## Context

Lifetime Club termination was surfaced as a decision item in the 4/15 Board Meeting (see D14 brief, meeting 2026-04-15). The program is an indefinite-duration, pre-paid benefit that lives outside the Recharge subscription model; continued liability exposure without accounting recognition is the core concern. No current wind-down plan, comms plan, member accounting, or regulatory review exists.

This is an **investigation** ChangeSet. The deliverable is a wind-down plan brief, not an execution. An Implementation ChangeSet will spawn from the findings once Peter + Josh determine the recommended path (honor-in-perpetuity vs. structured sunset vs. voluntary buyout).

## Problem

Lifetime Club members have unbounded future claims against Kenetik at a fixed (or zero) per-unit cost. As volume scales, this becomes a balance-sheet liability the company cannot easily quantify, has not modeled cash flow impact for, and has not disclosed to members as an active program or a closed one. Board flagged as material to the $5M investor round.

## Hypothesis

A structured wind-down — new enrollments closed, existing members honored under a defined benefit schedule, cohort accounted for as deferred revenue — protects member goodwill while capping the liability and resolving the investor-round blocker.

## Deliverables

1. Member cohort size, enrollment history, SKU entitlement mapping (Josh)
2. Historical and projected cash impact per member-year (Josh)
3. Legal review of termination clauses in original program terms (Peter)
4. Options analysis: (a) honor-in-perpetuity, (b) structured 24/36/60-month sunset, (c) voluntary buyout at cash value (Peter + Josh)
5. Member comms plan per selected option, with brand voice alignment (Peter, coordinated with Charlotte for tone)
6. Accounting treatment recommendation for Cin7 + QuickBooks (Josh)
