## §10a Hypothesis

Both `/pages/ashton-hall` (111 sessions/wk, 0% CVR, 96.4% bounce) and `/pages/kenetik-vs-other-ketones` (276 sessions/wk, 0% CVR, 56.9% bounce) are landing pages without conversion pathways. Ashton Hall is functionally broken as a conversion surface; /vs-other-ketones lets visitors read the comparison content (mid-50s bounce indicates engagement) but provides no purchase CTA. Per Resolution Hierarchy Step 3 (Refine), extending the collection-page conversion pattern that already converts at 4.91% CVR (`/collections/kenetik-ketone-products`) onto these two surfaces is the cheapest, highest-confidence intervention. **Hypothesis:** Adding standardized CTA placement, primary above-the-fold buy CTA, secondary product module, and inline row CTAs on `/vs-other-ketones` will lift both pages to ≥1.95% CVR (50% of site avg of 3.90%) within 7 days of deploy. Critical timing: Ashton Hall is a 5/13 GP-launch destination (Ashton Hall Briefcase Reveal); /vs-other-ketones is the consolidating evidence page for the BHB-vs-goBHB comparison (CS-139). Failing to ship before 5/13 compounds revenue loss across all five 5/13 partner activations and weakens the CS-139 narrative.

## §10b Resolution Path (Diagnose / Remove / Refine / Add)

- **Diagnose:** Ashton Hall: page either lacks a CTA entirely OR has a rendering/layout bug suppressing conversions; D09 SIG-02 confirms 0% CVR for two consecutive measurement windows. /vs-other-ketones: bounce is reasonable (56.9%) so visitors are reading the content; the failure mode is "no purchase pathway after engagement."
- **Remove:** Nothing to remove on Ashton Hall (already minimal); on /vs-other-ketones, do not remove comparison content (it's working at the engagement layer).
- **Refine:** Extend the `/collections/kenetik-ketone-products` template (4.91% CVR, 37.2% bounce -- best-performing top-10 LP). Apply: (a) primary "Shop Kenetik" CTA above the fold; (b) sticky CTA on scroll; (c) inline CTAs after each comparison row on /vs-other-ketones; (d) destination link to `/collections/kenetik-ketone-products` from /vs-other-ketones; (e) verify mobile rendering on iOS Safari + Android Chrome end-to-end.
- **Add:** Not needed -- this is purely a Refine. New template construction was rejected as Resolution Hierarchy Step 4 because the existing collection pattern is sufficient.
