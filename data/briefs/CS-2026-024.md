## Hypothesis
"Too much product" accounts for 23% of voluntary churn (12/52 churns in trailing 7d). Introducing a prominent pause/skip option in the subscription management flow and pre-churn touchpoints will reduce voluntary churn by ≥15% while retaining LTV, as subscribers who pause are 3-5x more likely to resume than those who cancel.

## Evidence
- Domain 06: 52 churns in trailing 7d. Voluntary 44% (23 churns).
- Domain 06: "Too much product" (12 churns, 23%) and "Too expensive" (12 churns, 23%) are top reasons.
- Domain 06: Active subscribers 1,781. Net growth +71 (healthy).
- Domain 06: Subscription AOV recovered to $43.43 (+102% from $21.44).
- Domain 06: Mar 15 spike (37 new subs) suggests CS-2026-007 approval driving higher-value subscriptions.
- Industry benchmark: Pause/skip reduces cancellation by 15-30% in DTC subscription models.

## Scope
1. **Recharge Configuration (Days 1-3):** Enable skip-next-charge and pause (30/60/90-day) options in Recharge subscription portal.
2. **Pre-Cancel Intercept (Days 4-7):** Add pause/skip prompt in cancellation flow before final cancel confirmation. Copy: "Getting too much? Skip your next shipment or pause for up to 90 days — no commitment."
3. **Proactive Outreach (Days 7-14):** Klaviyo flow targeting subscribers with 2+ consecutive charges: "Need a break? Pause anytime — we’ll be here when you’re ready."
4. **SMS Touchpoint (Days 7-14):** Add pause/skip reminder to subscription lifecycle SMS (connects to rec-015 SMS expansion).

### What Was Accomplished
- **Scope item 1 (Recharge Configuration):** Already complete. The Recharge subscription portal already has prominent "Skip," "Reschedule," and "Send now" options visible to customers on their account dashboard. No additional configuration was needed — this was live before the ChangeSet was created.

### Gap Resolution
- **Gap 1 (Recharge Pause/Skip State):** Resolved. Screenshot confirmed skip and reschedule are live in the customer portal. The feature-enablement scope was unnecessary.
- **Gap 2 (Voluntary vs Involuntary Churn Data):** Resolved. Voluntary churn data (including cancellation reasons) is accessible in Recharge via: Churn tool > Cancellation Prevents > Performance > Cancellation Survey Response. This data source can support future measurement plans.
- **Gap 3 (Scope Update):** Moot — ChangeSet closed rather than revised (see rationale below).

### Why the Remaining Scope Was Not Built
- **Scope items 2–4 (Pre-cancel intercept, proactive Klaviyo flow, SMS touchpoint):** These items required identifying subscribers who are "accumulating too much product" for proactive outreach. There is no reliable behavioral signal in Recharge or Klaviyo to identify these subscribers before they decide to cancel — skip history indicates awareness of the feature, not accumulation risk, and tenure/frequency alone is too noisy to target effectively.
- **Churn risk from added touchpoints:** Adding net-new email and SMS flows to the subscriber lifecycle increases communication frequency, which itself can be a churn driver. The risk of annoying subscribers with "do you need a break?" messaging outweighs the uncertain benefit of catching pre-churn subscribers who haven't yet self-identified.

### Successor ChangeSet
A new ChangeSet will be created to optimize the existing upcoming order notification emails — embedding skip/reschedule CTAs and value-reinforcing content into touchpoints subscribers already expect, rather than introducing new ones. This addresses the same churn-reduction goal through a lower-risk, higher-signal channel.

### Learnings
1. Always confirm current feature state before scoping implementation work — the portal already had the core capability.
2. Proactive churn-prevention flows require a reliable targeting signal; without one, optimizing existing touchpoints is the better path.
3. Cancellation survey data in Recharge (Churn tool) is a viable data source for voluntary churn segmentation — document this for future ChangeSet measurement plans.
