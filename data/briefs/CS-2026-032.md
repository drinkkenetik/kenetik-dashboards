## Problem Statement

Domain 06 (Session 14) identified 100 Recharge payment processing failures in the trailing 7 days — nearly 2x voluntary cancellations (51). Involuntary churn from payment failures is the #1 headwind to subscription growth despite strong activation momentum (+40% WoW, 136 new activations). Reactivation rate is critically low at 2.2% (3 reactivations in 7 days vs industry benchmark 5-10%), meaning failed subscribers are being permanently lost.

## Hypothesis

Auditing and remediating top payment failure causes (declined cards, expired methods, 3D Secure, gateway timeouts) combined with a targeted payment recovery automation will reduce involuntary churn by 50% and recover 50+ subscribers per week.

## Data Basis

- **Payment failures (7d):** 100 events (avg 12.5/day) — Domain 06, Mar 13-20
- **Prior week failures:** 110 (avg 15.7/day) — down 9% but still elevated
- **Voluntary cancellations:** 51/week (payment failures = 2x voluntary)
- **Reactivation rate:** 2.2% (3 events vs 136 activations) — critically below 5-10% benchmark
- **Net subscriber change:** +85 (would be +135 if 50% of failures recovered)
- **Active subscriptions:** 1,077 (from Session 13 cached metrics)

## Actions

### Phase 1: Root Cause Audit (Days 1-3)
1. Pull Recharge charge processing logs for failure reason breakdown
2. Categorize: declined cards, expired payment methods, 3D Secure rejections, gateway timeouts, insufficient funds
3. Check payment processor settings and any recent integration changes
4. Identify top 3 failure reasons by volume

### Phase 2: Infrastructure Fix (Days 4-7)
1. Implement card updater service (if not already active)
2. Review and optimize dunning/retry logic in Recharge
3. Fix any gateway configuration issues identified in audit

### Phase 3: Recovery Automation (Days 5-10)
1. Design win-back SMS/email flow for payment failure subscribers
2. Offer: 10-15% discount on reactivation
3. Deploy via Klaviyo triggered by Recharge payment failure event
4. Target: 10%+ reactivation rate (from 2.2% baseline)

## Risk Assessment

- **Low risk (Phase 1-2):** Audit and infrastructure fixes are operational improvements
- **Medium risk (Phase 3):** Discount offer could cannibalize full-price reactivations
- **Guardrail:** Cap discount at 15%; limit to 1 offer per subscriber per 90 days
- **Rollback:** Disable recovery flow if discount redemption >25% of new subscriptions
