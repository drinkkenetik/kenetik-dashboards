## Risk Assessment

- **Reversibility:** HIGH. Maverick is a vendor integration with Klaviyo. Test-end rollback = disconnect integration, revert to standard Klaviyo campaign sends. No irreversible configuration changes.
- **Guardrails breach risk:** LOW-MEDIUM. Maverick operates at the deliverability layer — modifying headers, authentication, or subject lines poorly can trigger spam filters or confuse reputable ESPs. Mitigation: the A/B structure itself is the safety mechanism — control arm is current send; any treatment-side deliverability issue is contained to the treatment slice, not the whole audience. Pull spam-complaint + bounce rates per campaign and halt test if guardrails are breached on 2 consecutive campaigns.
- **Brand voice risk:** LOW. If Maverick auto-generates subject-line or preheader variants, Katie must visually approve before send — do not let the tool A/B brand copy without review. Confirm Maverick's scope of modifications at Step 1 of evaluation (see Publish Plan).
- **Attribution risk:** MEDIUM. Vendor A/B reporting may not reconcile to Klaviyo-native or Polar-native attribution. Mitigation: cross-validate Maverick's treatment-vs-control revenue claims against Klaviyo campaign reports weekly.
- **Sample risk:** MEDIUM. 30 days × ~3 campaigns/week = ~13 campaigns. Depending on Maverick's A/B split and per-campaign audience size, statistical power for detecting a <20% lift may be marginal. If at Day 14 mid-test check the test is underpowered, extend measurement window by 15 days rather than forcing a conclusion.
- **Collision risk with CS-2026-035 (SMS-First promotional strategy experiment, Katie-owned, published):** MEDIUM. CS-2026-035 tests 4-hour SMS lead time before email on promotional sends. Both experiments modify the same promotional email campaigns. Mitigation: document overlapping campaigns explicitly in both tests; at analysis stage, segment Maverick results by whether the campaign was also under CS-2026-035 SMS-first treatment. If segmented n is too small, flag that as an analysis limitation rather than halting either test.

## Rollback Plan

**Mid-test halt conditions (any triggers rollback within 24h):**
1. Unsubscribe rate on Maverick treatment arm exceeds 1.5× control on 2 consecutive campaigns.
2. Spam complaint rate exceeds control + 0.01pp absolute on any single campaign.
3. Deliverability / bounce rate exceeds 1.25× control on 2 consecutive campaigns.
4. Katie identifies a brand-voice breach in Maverick-generated subject/preheader variants.

**Rollback mechanism:** Disable Maverick integration in Klaviyo admin (single toggle per vendor docs — confirm at Step 1). All future sends revert to control configuration. No customer-facing rollback communication needed; A/B test structure means no customers received a degraded experience en masse.

**Test-end decision tree:**
- **Scale (retain Maverick):** Primary metric ≥+30% RPR lift, all guardrails clean, attribution cross-validates within 5%.
- **Iterate (retain + renegotiate / narrow scope):** Primary metric +10% to +29% RPR lift, guardrails clean. Renegotiate software pricing or restrict Maverick to highest-volume campaigns only.
- **Discontinue:** Primary metric <+10% lift, OR any guardrail breached, OR attribution fails cross-validation. Disable integration at test-end.
