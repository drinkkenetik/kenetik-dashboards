## Creative Brief

### Subject lines (3 — A/B/C with 4h winner-selection window, Klaviyo 20/20/60 split)
  1. **"3 Monsters down to 1 Kenetik."** — angle: social-proof/contrast · rationale: Specific number, contrast frame. Wedge B incumbent-habit-replacement.
  2. **"She quit the cans. (Not those cans.)"** — angle: curiosity/contrast · rationale: Curiosity + parenthetical hook. Aligns with 'contrast not attack' rule.
  3. **"Calm clarity, no crash."** — angle: validator · rationale: Pure validator language. Subtle, Wedge B-targeted.

### Preview text (paired with subject variants)
  1. Real customers. Real swaps. Real focus.
  2. What changed when they stopped chasing the next Red Bull.
  3. No jitters. No crash. Just clarity.

### Body direction
Open with one anchor story: a customer (or composite) who used to drink 2–3 Monsters/NOS/Red Bulls daily. What changed when they switched to Kenetik. First-person where permissioned, otherwise aggregate. NEVER call Kenetik an 'energy drink.' Contrast framing only. Tier A optional sidebar: Korsak as backdrop. Banned: 'addiction,' 'cravings,' 'withdrawal,' 'reduces anxiety.' Validator language: no jitters, no crash, sleep protection, calm clarity.

### Science references
- **Korsak 2026 (PMID 41773904)** — 31-day KME supplementation significantly improved cognitive function (Stroop test, p=0.015) in trained runners — cognitive gains occurred independently of running performance changes, supporting ketones as a brain-specific fuel.
