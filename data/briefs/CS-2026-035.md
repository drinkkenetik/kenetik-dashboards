## Hypothesis

Shifting to SMS-first sends (4 hours before email) for promotional campaigns will increase campaign click rate by 3x and revenue per recipient by 2x.

## Evidence

- BAW SMS achieved 27.7% click rate versus email campaigns at 0.68% -- a 40x differential (D02 SIG-02-C)
- Welcome Series SMS converts at 11.5% (D02 what_worked)
- pat-003 (SMS Channel Preference) now has 3 consecutive data points confirming SMS dominance for promotional content
- Matt Hughes Story email was the exception at 48.6% open, $9,595 revenue -- narrative format may be the differentiator, not the channel

## Measurement Plan

**Test Design:** Next 3 promotional campaigns (non-narrative format):
- **Treatment:** SMS send 4 hours before email send for same offer
- **Control:** Simultaneous SMS + email send (current protocol)
- **Split:** 50/50 audience split using Klaviyo segments

**KPIs:**
**Department:** lifecycle-marketing
**Platforms:** [klaviyo]
1. Click rate (primary) -- target: SMS 3x email baseline
2. Revenue per recipient (primary) -- target: 2x email baseline
3. Unsubscribe rate (guardrail) -- must not exceed 1.5x current rate
4. Cross-channel cannibalization -- monitor email open rate in treatment group

**Baseline (from BAW data):**
- Email campaign click rate: 0.68%
- SMS click rate: 27.7%
- Email revenue/campaign: ~$9,400 (BAW email)
- SMS revenue/campaign: ~$3,600 (BAW SMS)

**Measurement Window:** 14 days from first send (target: 2026-04-06)

## Expected Impact

- **Revenue uplift:** $2,000-4,000 per promotional campaign from improved click-to-purchase rate
- **L1 Margin impact:** Positive -- SMS has lower per-send cost than email for Kenetik's volume
- **Risk:** Low. SMS list is already active. Experiment is fully reversible.

## Implementation Steps

1. Katie creates Klaviyo experiment segments (50/50 split)
2. Katie configures next promotional campaign with 4h SMS lead time for treatment group
3. Monitor first send within 24h for unsubscribe guardrail
4. Repeat for 2 additional campaigns
5. Aggregate results and determine if SMS-first becomes default
