## 1. Summary

Optimize the existing upcoming subscription order notification email to reduce voluntary churn by reinforcing product value and surfacing skip/reschedule options — using an existing touchpoint subscribers already expect rather than adding new flows.

## 1b. Resolution Path

| Step | Considered? | Result |
|------|------------|--------|
| **Diagnose** | "Too much product" accounts for 23% of voluntary churn per Recharge cancellation survey data. Subscribers who accumulate excess product cancel rather than using skip/reschedule — likely because the existing upcoming order notification doesn't remind them these options exist. | Root cause: the pre-charge email is transactional/bare, missing value reinforcement and self-service CTAs. |
| **Remove** | Yes | Could remove friction by auto-pausing low-engagement subscribers, but this is too aggressive and removes revenue from healthy subscribers. |
| **Refine** | Yes | The upcoming order notification already exists (likely sent by Recharge natively — Klaviyo flow "UE \| Upcoming Subscription Order Flow" exists but shows zero sends in last 365 days). Refining this existing touchpoint is the right path: add value content + skip/reschedule CTAs to an email subscribers already receive. No new touchpoints needed. |
| **Add** | No | CS-2026-024 originally proposed adding proactive "need a break?" flows, but there's no reliable signal to identify at-risk subscribers before they self-select, and adding touchpoints increases churn risk. |

**Resolution Path: Refine**

## 2. Objective & Impact Projection

Reduce voluntary churn from "too much product" by transforming the upcoming order notification from a bare transactional email into a value-reinforcing touchpoint with clear self-service options. Primary objective level: **L3 — Customer Quality / LTV** (retention improvement).

| Level | Expected Impact | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| L1 — Contribution Margin | +$1,200–$2,400/mo (prevented churn) | 12 churns/week from "too much product." If 15-25% of those are prevented by skip/reschedule adoption → 7-13 subscribers retained/month × $43.43 AOV × remaining LTV months. Conservative: ~$1,200/mo margin at 50% DTC. |
| L2 — Cash Conversion | Positive | Retained subscribers = recurring DTC revenue with predictable cash timing vs. re-acquisition spend. |
| L3 — Customer Quality / LTV | Positive — subscribers who skip and return have higher lifetime value than those who cancel and are re-acquired. Pausers resume at 3-5x the rate of cancellers (industry benchmark). | L3 reinforces L1: subscription LTV compounds monthly while re-acquisition costs $15-30 CAC. |
| L4 — Rate of Learning | What content in the upcoming order email drives skip/reschedule adoption vs. cancellation? Does value reinforcement (science/benefits) in a transactional touchpoint affect churn rate? | First test of embedding retention messaging in existing transactional flows. |
| L5 — Brand Equity | Pass — no discounting, no health claims requiring review. Value messaging uses approved Tier A language. Skip/reschedule is a customer-service improvement. |

**Design:** Clean, on-brand template consistent with existing UE flows. Single-column, mobile-first.
