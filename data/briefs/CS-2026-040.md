## Problem Statement
Creator dormancy is at 88.8% (222 of 250 creators generated zero conversions in the last 5 weeks, Mar 1 to Apr 7). Only 28 unique codes drove any revenue. Program revenue is heavily concentrated in equity partners: CLEETUS ($2,385), SMILEMORE ($2,056), ROMAN ($921), DOC ($587). Below the equity tier, Premier Partners generated $774 across 9 active codes, Affiliates generated $972 across 7 active codes, and Brand Champions had a single $32 conversion. The remaining 222 creators are completely dormant.

This changeset was originally drafted Mar 25 and sat in draft for 12 days. The April code boost schedule (Brand Champions Apr 13, Premier Partners Apr 20, Affiliates Apr 27) creates a natural reactivation runway. This revised plan aligns the 3-tier outreach with those code boost windows so creators are re-engaged BEFORE their tier's boost goes live.

## Hypothesis
A structured 3-tier reactivation campaign, sequenced ahead of the April code boosts, can reactivate 20-25 dormant creators within 30 days by reaching creators at the right intensity based on their tier and historical value. The code boost provides a tangible reason to re-engage ("your code is about to get a 25% discount boost, here's how to use it").

- **Tier 1 (Personal outreach, this week Apr 7-11):** Brand Champions who already have Stress Awareness Month content briefs. Nick owns outreach and planning for this tier, re-engaging them before the Apr 13-17 code boost.
- **Tier 2 (Warm outreach, Apr 14-18):** Premier Partners. Nick, Lawrence, and Derek tag-team outreach, ship product where needed, and give creators time to film before the Apr 20-24 code boost.
- **Tier 3 (Broad outreach, Apr 14-25):** Affiliates. Lawrence owns this tier, using a Superfiliate email sequence to re-engage the full affiliate base before the Apr 27 code boost. This is the highest-volume, lowest-touch tier.

Target: 20-25 new active creators (conversions or content posted) by May 7.

## Execution Plan

### Tier 1: Brand Champions (Apr 7-11)
**Owner:** Nick
**Target:** All Brand Champions without a conversion in the last 30 days
**Why first:** They already have Stress Awareness Month content briefs from this week's batch. They have direction. They just need a nudge.

Actions:
1. Nick pulls the full Brand Champion roster from Superfiliate
2. Cross-reference against the 28 active codes to identify dormant Brand Champions
3. Nick sends personal DM or text to each dormant Brand Champion:
   - "Hey [name], wanted to make sure you saw your Stress Awareness content brief. Your code is getting a 25% discount boost starting Apr 13. Great time to post and drive some sales."

4. For any Brand Champion who responds positively, ship product if they need it (coordinate with warehouse)

6. Track responses, content posted, conversions during Apr 20-24 boost

### Tier 3: Affiliates (Apr 14-25)
**Owner:** Lawrence
**Target:** All Affiliates without a conversion in the last 30 days
**Why third:** This is the largest group (180+ creators) and needs the most lead time. The Apr 27 boost date was specifically set to give Lawrence time to work through the list.

Actions:
**Owner:** Lawrence
1. Lawrence pulls the full Affiliate roster
2. Cross-reference against active codes (7 active: BREAKTHROUGH, BWELLZ15, IVREAL, VIGEO15, NESLO, BOOBSOVERBARS, SETHK)

## Risks
- Bandwidth is distributed: Nick owns Tier 1, the three-person team splits Tier 2, and Lawrence handles Tier 3 solo via Superfiliate email. This is manageable.
- Low-quality reactivations: creators post once during the boost and go dormant again. Mitigation: track 30-day retention, not just initial reactivation.
- Some enrolled creators may be dead accounts (wrong email, never intended to post, signed up for the discount only). The audit will surface these.
- Product sampling budget not confirmed for Tier 2 shipments.

## Learning from March Code Boost
The March code boost (CS completed) showed that boosts work well for already-engaged creators but do NOT re-engage dormant creators. This changeset addresses that gap directly: re-engage BEFORE the boost, so creators are primed and ready when the discount goes live.
