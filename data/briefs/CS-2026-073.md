## Hypothesis

Winback flow is clicking at 0.92% across 1,977 recipients — healthier than Expected To Purchase but well below potential. A more aggressive offer variant (e.g., free shipping on 2-pack or 20% off first subscription) will lift click-to-conversion without structural flow changes. SMS mirror (tested against email) will isolate channel effect given the 15.6x Sea Otter SMS/email delta.

## Actions

1. Design 2 email variants: (A) current offer (control), (B) stronger offer (20% off first subscription order).
2. Mirror top-performing variant to SMS 24h after email send.
3. Run 14-day hold-out with 50/50 split, 500 recipients per arm minimum.
4. Report lift in clicks, conversion, revenue per recipient, and incrementality vs. control.

## Success Criteria

- Variant B drives ≥2x conversion rate vs. control.
- SMS mirror drives ≥5x click rate vs. email variant (baseline delta confirmation).
- No cannibalization of next-flow conversion (subscription sign-up net of winback).
