## Hypothesis

CP-012 motorsports/Cleetus content holds engagement when graduated from organic TT to paid TT. If paid TT amplification holds ≥3% engagement at ≥3x organic reach with ≥1.5x ROAS in 14 days, we unlock a second TT graduation channel beyond Matt Hughes day-in-the-life (CP-013) and prove the motorsports lane generalizes across creator (Cleetus + Cam Jones EP-009) and event (Rockingham, Freedom 500, Sea Otter Unbound).

## Recommended Paid Setup (for Katie's scoping)

- **Objective:** Mix — 60% traffic/conversion (pixel ROAS), 40% video views (TT-native reach extension)
- **Creative format:** Vertical TT-native cuts (do NOT reformat FB reel). Use existing TT post creatives as paid assets via Spark Ads where possible.
- **Audience:** TT interest-based — motorsports / NASCAR / racing fans; broad TT lookalike from past 90d converters; retargeting from KME engagement.
- **Budget:** Start at $50/day per ad set, 3 ad sets (interests, LAL, retargeting). 14-day window. Pause anything <2x ROAS or <2% engagement at day 7.

## Measurement Plan

- **Primary:** 14d ROAS ≥ 1.5x AND TT engagement ≥ 3% on paid placements.
- **Secondary:** Cost per click, video view-through rate, save rate on TT paid.
- **Hold-out criteria:** If 7-day ROAS <1.0x AND engagement <2% on all ad sets, rollback to reach-extension objective only and document as EP-012 paid-surface fatigue.
