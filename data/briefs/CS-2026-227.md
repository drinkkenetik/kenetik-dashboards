## Problem Statement

26 subscribers churned involuntarily in June 2026 -- a card declined and nobody recovered them
[src: kenetik-growth-system/data/subscriptions/subscription-state.json#recharge_monthly.2026-06,
field passive_churned]. A six-week cancellation-reason log counted 44 "Failed Payment Flow max
retries" events over a longer, overlapping window [src: data/voc/cancellations-corpus.json],
directionally consistent. These customers did not choose to leave; nobody asked them to fix a
declined card before the subscription lapsed. Prior related work (`CS-2026-032`, "Payment Failure
Root Cause Audit & Recovery Program," created 2026-03-20) targeted this same problem at a much
smaller subscriber base (1,077 active subs at the time) and is not present in the current
tracker.json (likely archived during the tracker's v3 migration) -- this CS refreshes the scope
against the current base of 2,447 subscribers rather than assuming that work is still live or
complete.

## Hypothesis

A card-updater network (automatic background refresh of expired/reissued card numbers), a
spaced retry schedule (roughly day 1/3/5/7 instead of same-day repeated attempts), and a
Klaviyo-triggered win-back sequence for subscribers who do lapse involuntarily will recover
25-40% of the ~26-44 monthly involuntary-churn segment (7-15 subscribers/month), at zero
incremental CAC.

## Resolution Path (Diagnose / Remove / Refine / Add)

- **Diagnose:** Involuntary churn is a plumbing failure (no recovery attempt reaches the
  customer before the subscription lapses), not a persuasion failure. No product or pricing
  change is implicated.
- **Remove:** N/A -- nothing in the current flow is actively causing harm; the gap is an absence
  of a recovery mechanism, not a broken one.
- **Refine:** Recharge's dunning/retry settings and card-updater feature (if not already
  enabled) are the existing mechanism this refines -- most Recharge plans include this natively;
  the fix is configuration, not new infrastructure.
- **Add:** A Klaviyo win-back sequence for subscribers who do lapse after retries are exhausted
  -- justified because Recharge's native retry/card-updater flow does not include a
  customer-facing communication sequence; Klaviyo is the correct tool for that messaging layer.

## Actions

### Phase 1: Recharge configuration audit (days 1-3)
1. Confirm whether card-updater network is active on the current Recharge plan; enable if not.
2. Set retry schedule to roughly day 1, 3, 5, 7 post-decline (verify against current plan
   capabilities -- some retry cadence customization may require a plan-tier check).
3. Confirm the Recharge "subscription cancelled -- passive/payment" event is wired to fire a
   Klaviyo trigger (verify integration, do not assume).

### Phase 2: Klaviyo dunning + win-back build (days 3-10)
Draft copy (brand-voice-checked, no health/performance claims -- see Claims Compliance below):

**Touch 1 (decline day, email + SMS):** "Your card didn't go through -- here's the easy fix" --
plain notice, one-click update-payment link, explicit "no charge has been made" reassurance.

**Touch 2 (day 3, email):** "Still on file: one declined charge" -- second reminder, lower
urgency, same CTA.

**Touch 3 (day 7, final retry window, email + SMS):** "Last try before your subscription
pauses" -- states the consequence plainly (pause, not silent loss), offers update-card or
pause-instead as equal options.

**Touch 4 (win-back, 14 days post-passive-cancel, email):** "We kept your subscription price" --
offers reinstatement at the same price/frequency, no re-signup friction, 30-day window.

## Risk Assessment

- **Risk:** Multi-platform (Recharge config + Klaviyo flow) -- coordination risk if retry-cadence
  changes and the Klaviyo trigger are not sequenced together (a Klaviyo win-back that fires before
  Recharge retries are exhausted would be confusing/premature).
- **Mitigation:** Sequence Phase 1 (Recharge) fully before Phase 2 (Klaviyo) build; verify the
  trigger event fires only after retries are exhausted, not on the first decline.
- **Rollback:** Disable the Klaviyo flow trigger; Recharge retry/card-updater settings can be
  reverted independently without affecting the flow build.
